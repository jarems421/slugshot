## Confirmed - useful distinctions, not novelty for its own sake, 2026-11-04

Owner feedback on Frostcrawler: a familiar, good impact ability is preferable to a complicated unprecedented mechanic. Compare actual outcomes across the roster, not just different elements or visuals. Arachnet already owns a brief movement bind; avoid adding more ground-sticking/freezing mains by default. This is a roster-diversity constraint, not a blanket ban on every slow or future ice effect. Frostcrawler's Ice Wall is approved; its main remains open after two rejected comparisons. Proposals must remain proposals until rated/selected.

## Confirmed - design abilities across the roster

- **Plan the roster before committing to an isolated ability.** Inferno's ability needs to take account of all, or at least the majority, of the intended slugs, even before the other creatures have visual designs. Establish provisional mechanics and relationships first; refine individual kits together.
- **Player-hit and passive surface behaviours per slug.** The latest owner clarification below expands the earlier one-ability wording: plan a player-hit effect and a passive effect triggered by hitting the environment. The hit target chooses the outcome automatically; there is no pre-shot ability selection. Creative use still provides depth, and the five Inferno studies were alternatives, not five simultaneously equipped powers.
- **Sequential chains matter.** A slug changes the situation and another exploits that change through a later shot or a teammate's action. This is the interaction being requested now. A twin-blast gun that combines two slugs into one attack is a separate future idea; no release date or implementation is approved here.
- **Interactions include elements, position and terrain.** Slugs should be able to work well together through environmental effects, elemental relationships and the positions of opponents. Depth should come from intelligent placement, timing, aim and order of use, beyond simply adding damage.
- **The roster is broader than terrain effects.** Some slugs can focus on direct damage or pressure; others on terrain or support, including healing. The owner's comparisons to fraggers and healers describe possible roles; role names and fixed classes have not been selected.
- **Inferno's impact burn is a shared baseline direction.** The owner wants Inferno's ability alternatives to burn the opponent on impact, giving fire pressure independently of the signature effect. This does not mean every species burns opponents. The exact damage, duration and resulting player response remain unchosen.
- **Contact matters for Inferno's signature effect.** The brief starts with a shot, a spin and transformation into the big form, then an ability triggered by impact. The fire-breath example below also illustrated an approach effect; it does not mandate pre-impact damage for every candidate. Preserve the existing transformation presentation unless separately revised.

## Confirmed - player-hit effect and passive surface ability

**Owner clarification, 2026-11-04:** plan two outcomes for a slug's shot: its ability on hitting a person, and its environmental effect on hitting terrain. The owner calls the latter the **passive ability**. Terrain includes **floors/ground, walls and ceilings**, not just horizontal ground.

- **Player hit:** activates the slug's player-hit effect. Inferno retains confirmed Backdraft, including impact burn and the directional pressure burst.
- **Surface hit:** activates that slug's passive ability at the struck surface. Its element/effect belongs to the species; the owner's fire wording refers to Inferno, not every slug.
- **Terminology:** "passive" means an effect triggered by an environmental impact here. It does not mean an always-on aura, an effect that appears merely in flight, or another manually selected power.
- **Reason for the split:** it gives the player-hit ability more design freedom while preserving environmental setup, control and cross-slug chains. Plan both outcomes together across the roster.

**Inferno passive confirmed, owner follow-up, 2026-11-04:** a shot hitting a floor, wall or ceiling creates a fire effect on that struck surface, and the fire burns players exposed to it. This is now the selected passive behaviour, not just an unselected example. Inferno keeps Backdraft as its player-hit effect. Exact shape/radius, lifetime, burn damage, exposure/reach, attachment technique and any spread, falling effects or propagation remain unchosen. Other species' passive effects remain open.

This clarification supersedes treating a slug's player-hit and terrain outcomes as competing choices that require selecting only one. It does not select Aquabeek's kit or approve native implementation. Existing concept studies need to be reviewed for their player-hit and floor/wall/ceiling outcomes before being treated as complete proposals.

## Confirmed - Inferno uses Backdraft

**Owner, 2026-11-04:** "backdraft. Let's set that in stone as the inferno ability." This supersedes the earlier pause on selecting Inferno's signature effect.

The selected core is **impact burn followed by a readable, delayed pressure vent along the incoming shot direction**, damaging/pushing opponents who remain in its path. Shot angle makes positional setup possible. Exact damage, burn duration, delay, cone width, push strength, immunity/stacking rules and production effects remain to be tuned. The browser study is an illustration, not Roblox implementation or balance evidence.

The owner requested keeping only Backdraft among the Inferno options in the local ability viewer. Other Inferno studies remain historical alternatives, not additional equipped powers. Gas plus knockback is still an illustrative cross-slug chain: choosing Backdraft does not select Flatulorhinkus's kit or any other slug's interaction rules.

## Considering - historical Inferno concept feedback

The original local animated studies explored Cinderwake, Backdraft, Flashover, Kiln Guard and Ash Bloom. Backdraft has now been selected as described above; the others were not chosen.

- **Owner preference:** Cinderwake, Backdraft and Ash Bloom were the most appealing. The owner liked damage/pressure, smart positioning plays and sight control respectively.
- **Owner criticism:** Cinderwake felt somewhat basic, particularly once impact burning was understood as common to Inferno's alternatives. Do not mistake the initial interest for a final choice.
- **Backdraft, history:** directional displacement was the assistant's recommended candidate. The owner first paused selection to establish the roster, then explicitly chose Backdraft. Do not carry forward the earlier "not selected" state.
- **Other studies:** Flashover's fire around connected cover and Kiln Guard's ally protection/vent were presented alternatives; the owner did not select them. Ash Bloom's obscuring plume and Cinderwake's movement trail are likewise provisional mechanics.

Suggested design checks from the discussion: usefulness on its own, several possible partners, readable opportunities to escape or interrupt a chain, and consistent rules for effects that strengthen, move, change or remove one another. These are planning criteria; no generic interaction engine, universal elemental rules or specific counter mechanics have been approved.

# Blender creature lessons — first development cycle

Updated 2026-10-31. This is a working record, not a redesign of the pipeline or approval of the creature. [Current design and verification](catalog/flopper-inspired.md). Preserve the owner’s feedback, the model source and the render that produced it; do not equate a passing script with a good design.

## Artistic and visual lessons

| Problem and known cause | Attempts and current response | Prevention and verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The owner rejected the [first draft](evidence/lessons/01-first-draft-rejected.png) as hard/blocky, with an oversized head, bulging eyes and unwanted feet. The draft visibly used separate, raised eye/foot forms. | Reworked it into one rounded body, fitted the spiral eyes to the surface and removed feet. | Review silhouette and eye depth in front, side and three-quarter views before adding detail. **Verified:** surface-distance and no-feet checks pass in Blender; subjective softness remains the owner's call. |
| The [white, spotted version](evidence/lessons/02-spots-and-no-feet.png) looked too clean and its rounded base appeared to hover. The owner wanted sick/dizzy character, marks all around and a very short tail. | Added diffused green vertex colours, a flat spread contact patch and a short tail as a continuation of the body. The [grounded version](evidence/lessons/03-grounded-base.png) replaced the round underside. | Check rear/side coverage, contact and tail silhouette as well as the front. **Verified:** quadrant coverage, short-tail and sampled ground-contact checks pass offline; owner review and Studio rendering remain open. |
| Colour direction was missed repeatedly: white was too white; a guessed dirty yellow was also rejected. Lighting affects appearance, but the precise cause of the mismatch was not established. | Tried a warmer off-white, then a stronger guessed yellow. After rejection, sampled the pale cream from the user's original 3D reference and used neutral review lighting. The owner later requested darker, muddier green spots. | Compare an actual render with the provided reference under recorded lighting; don't treat numeric colour values as visual approval. **Verified:** reference colour was sampled and current colours survive Blender FBX reimport; exact visual match and Roblox appearance remain unverified. |
| The owner found the sides/head too straight or hard, and worried the head would look flat from the side. The [muted-spots revision](evidence/lessons/04-muted-spots-head.png) was described as looking nice, but the owner still requested more head depth and softness; that was not final approval. | Tried a gently wider head and narrower middle, then added a rounded forward forehead, deeper head profile, broad settled curves and small idle head deformation. | Make front **and side** views part of each shape review; check that added depth doesn't undo accepted proportions. **Verified:** geometry/deformation checks and Blender renders pass; softness and proportions need the owner's judgment. |
| The owner said antennae looked attached/stiff, wanted them a little forward and then reported a tip touching the forehead. Moving the image-right tip outward cleared it but created the [unequal-antenna revision](evidence/lessons/05-unequal-antennae.png), which the owner asked to make symmetrical. | Tried fused roots, local smoothing and graded weights. A fuller forehead later rejoined a tip; advancing it cleared the geometry, then mirroring the inward curve restored symmetry. | Check both tips in front/side/three-quarter views and against the animated head; use mirrored rest controls when symmetry is requested. **Verified:** connected skin, mirrored tip positions and sampled idle clearance pass in Blender; perceived clearance and final shape await owner review. |

**Recurring visual weakness:** repeated script-generated shape and proportion changes passed validation while the owner still saw hard materials, poor anatomy or wrong colour. That points to a limitation of relying on procedural primitives and technical checks as the art review. For the next creature, agree on silhouette/colour from multi-angle drafts early, then inspect deformation and only then build out details. This is a workflow lesson, not an authorisation to rebuild the pipeline.

## Technical lessons

| Problem and cause | Attempts and current response | Prevention and verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first headless preview failed because a generated empty scene had no World. | Added a preview World when absent. | Start preview setup by creating missing scene resources. **Verified:** repeated headless renders pass. |
| An attachment corruption test passed incorrectly because Blender's cached bounding box was stale after a mesh edit. | Switched attachment checks to bounds calculated from actual vertices. | Read current mesh geometry or force an update before relying on cached spatial data. **Verified:** the deliberate displaced-vertex test now fails as intended. |
| Generated source could otherwise be re-exported against changed configuration and receive a fresh-looking report. Fixed eye attachment offsets also failed after head geometry changed; the offsets required recalibration. | Added configuration/tool/rig fingerprints, source/export hashes and stale-source rejection; remeasured the surface-projected eye offsets when the head changed. | Regenerate after config/rig/tool changes; inspect surface attachment positions after reshaping. Fixed offsets are a recurring fragility: a future surface-derived attachment workflow may help, but is not implemented here. **Verified:** stale-source and displaced-attachment probes pass; future automation is unverified. |
| The fuller forehead caused an antenna tip to merge back into the head, creating a closed handle. Later, single-side clearance made the two curves visibly unequal. | Detected the handle with connected-skin/Euler checks, moved the tip clear, then used one mirrored curve for both sides and checked tip-to-forehead distance during sampled idle poses. | Test geometry and render several angles after head or appendage changes; check symmetry separately from 3D clearance. **Verified:** current Blender checks pass; Studio and owner visual review remain open. |
| Bone-scale squash/blink and green vertex colours have not been verified in Roblox. Blender FBX reimport does not establish target-engine behavior; Studio MCP exposed no connected instance. | Kept exportable clips/vertex colours and an isolated Studio checklist. Blender roundtrips preserved data; no Roblox import or animation playback was observed. | Complete a minimal authorised Studio import, colour, skinning and animation roundtrip before relying on the asset in-game. **Verified:** offline only; Roblox result unresolved. |
| Fusing soft antenna roots required a higher-detail review mesh, close to the per-mesh budget. No device benchmark or performance failure has been measured. | Used voxel fusion/smoothing and decimation within the documented export budget. | Once the visual design is approved, assess topology, mesh count and device performance before producing many creatures. **Verified:** offline counts/export pass; production performance is unverified. |

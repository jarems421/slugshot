# Blender creature lessons — first development cycle

Updated 2026-10-31. This is a working record, not a redesign of the pipeline or approval of the creature. [Current design and verification](catalog/flopper-inspired.md). Preserve the owner’s feedback, the model source and the render that produced it; do not equate a passing script with a good design.

| The owner said antennae looked attached/stiff, wanted them a little forward and then reported a tip touching the forehead. | The owner said the baseline was "way too solid and hard", the eyes "look glued on", and the mouth "doesn't look like it's got life to it". The cause was a near-cylindrical upright profile and flat stickers projected onto a curved head. | Changed method on a variant instead of nudging the baseline. The new profile has a long low foot and tail taper, a soft neck and a forward-slumped head. Eyes sit in flat recessed sockets with puffy lid shells, dark under-eye bags, and spirals spun by eye-bone rotation about each socket normal. First lids made as flat slabs looked like stuck-on plates; domed shells that sink into the socket rim fixed that. A thin, tight spiral read as a target; fewer, thicker turns read as a spiral. | Judge the face from close-up front frames at several idle times. A spin must happen in a flat socket plane, or the spiral leaves the surface. Blink scaling and spin must not share an action, or the squash axis rotates. **Verified:** variant source checks and renders only; owner verdict pending; small lid crease remains. |
| On the variant, the owner said the eyes "still look very much stuck on", one under-eye patch was misplaced, a second spiral ring poked out of an eye, the antennae looked stuck to the head, the tail was "way too long", and the body "looks so hard, it doesn't look squishy". Causes: flat eye discs; bags and sockets were computed per side from the uneven normals of the decimated mesh; the spiral's outer width went past the disc; antenna roots were narrow; a long back-swept foot; and a glossy coat. | Eyes are now shallow glossy domes in recessed sockets, and the lids are sphere shells slightly larger than the eyeball, sinking into the skin with colour sampled from nearby skin. One socket is measured and mirrored; face marks use mirrored front-view coordinates; the spiral stays inside the dome. The antenna roots flare from inside the head; the foot is rounder with a short tail tip; the body has a belly sag, neck roll, deeper warm scattering and sheen, plus a travelling jelly wobble and rebound. Thin lid shells rendered grey under deep scattering, so the lids use a copy of the skin material with scattering turned off. | Mirror one measured side instead of measuring each side of an asymmetric decimated mesh. Keep painted or geometric features inside the part they belong to, at every animation frame. Avoid scattering on thin shells. Squishiness here comes from shape (sag, rolls, soft contact) and motion; Roblox will not show Blender subsurface scattering or transmission. Research found mainly paid soft-body add-ons and lid-shell eye advice. **Verified:** variant source checks and renders only; owner verdict pending. |
| After the third variant pass, the owner said it "just looks horrible" and that none of the added squishiness was visible. They also reported a white outline under the eyes. A critique they shared names an unconvincing head/torso/tail relationship (an upright ghost from the front, a wedge from the side), a flat face lacking cheek and brow volumes, handle-like appendages, unstructured muddy markings, and a disconnected mouth and drool. The white outline is the eyeball's buried rim showing where the socket has no lower lid. The squish was 2–3% motion plus Blender-only material scattering, too subtle to read in the viewport. | No fix yet; the method itself is in question. Each pass has been a scripted adjustment to one lofted profile with painted spots and features added afterwards. | Treat this as the method limit already predicted in this record: agree on the silhouette and anatomy as 2D front/side drafts, or a hand or AI-assisted sculpt, before more procedural passes. Squash and wobble must be exaggerated (10–20%) to read. **Unresolved:** owner choice of method. |
| Building the approved concept in Blender: thin lid shells came out with sawtooth edges at a 9 mm sampling grid, vertex colours blurred the thin lip and drool outlines, and test renders looked washed out. | Ported the concept's signed-distance shape and colour code to NumPy. Sampled at 6.5 mm, meshed with surface nets, decimated, then re-projected onto the exact surface. Baked the colour per texel from rest position into a 2048 texture. Judged colour with standard tone mapping and moderate lights. | Agree the design as a live 3D concept first, then build from the same maths; that ended the cycle of rejected procedural passes. Use fine sampling plus decimation for thin features, and texture bakes for line detail. Treat render exposure as a separate concern from the model. **Verified:** Blender renders and loop/floor checks; owner check pending. |
| Roblox version of Floppy: Roblox bones animate by CFrame only, the source idle used bone scale for breathing, Roblox caps textures at 1024, and the source mesh is 360k triangles. | Exported an 8.8k-triangle copy with fresh UVs and colour and normal maps baked from the full-detail source; replaced the scale keys with small translations; turned the model to +Y for the project's -Z FBX convention. | Keep the game rig scale-free from the start, bake detail into normal and colour maps, and verify by FBX reimport (bones, influences, facing, loop seam, zero scale). **Verified:** offline reimport; Studio pending. |
| The loaded Floppy came out far smaller than planned (owner: "so much more space"): about 15% of ground size instead of about 40%. The likely cause is that the in-gun fit and centring used `Model:GetBoundingBox()`, which can be inflated on an imported skinned model. | Measure the slug from its bones instead: eye spacing gives the true scale, the source dimensions give the size, and the root bone gives the base centre. The fit now uses the body width without antenna tips, so the slug fills the canister. The test place prints the bounding box beside the true size for comparison. | Don't trust GetBoundingBox for sizing imported skinned meshes; derive size from bones or known source dimensions. **Unresolved:** confirm the printed sizes and the visual fit in Studio. |

**Recurring visual weakness:** repeated script-generated shape and proportion changes passed validation while the owner still saw hard materials, poor anatomy or wrong colour. In the 2026-10-31 tooling brief, the owner still described the current baseline as cylindrical, with a weak silhouette, attached-looking eyes/appendages and limited expression. These are unresolved artistic judgments, not failed Blender checks. Relying on procedural primitives and technical tests as art review is a likely workflow weakness; the precise geometric cause has not been established. For the next creature, agree on silhouette/colour from multi-angle drafts early, then inspect deformation and only then build out details. See [skill routing](skill-routing.md) for the future method-comparison procedure. This does not authorise a pipeline rebuild or overwrite of the baseline.

## Technical lessons

| Problem and cause | Attempts and current response | Prevention and verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first headless preview failed because a generated empty scene had no World. | Added a preview World when absent. | Start preview setup by creating missing scene resources. **Verified:** repeated headless renders pass. |
| An attachment corruption test passed incorrectly because Blender's cached bounding box was stale after a mesh edit. | Switched attachment checks to bounds calculated from actual vertices. | Read current mesh geometry or force an update before relying on cached spatial data. **Verified:** the deliberate displaced-vertex test now fails as intended. |
| Generated source could otherwise be re-exported against changed configuration and receive a fresh-looking report. Fixed eye attachment offsets also failed after head geometry changed; the offsets required recalibration. | Added configuration/tool/rig fingerprints, source/export hashes and stale-source rejection; remeasured the surface-projected eye offsets when the head changed. | Regenerate after config/rig/tool changes; inspect surface attachment positions after reshaping. Fixed offsets are a recurring fragility: a future surface-derived attachment workflow may help, but is not implemented here. **Verified:** stale-source and displaced-attachment probes pass; future automation is unverified. |
| The fuller forehead caused an antenna tip to merge back into the head, creating a closed handle. Later, single-side clearance made the two curves visibly unequal. | Detected the handle with connected-skin/Euler checks, moved the tip clear, then used one mirrored curve for both sides and checked tip-to-forehead distance during sampled idle poses. | Test geometry and render several angles after head or appendage changes; check symmetry separately from 3D clearance. **Verified:** current Blender checks pass; Studio and owner visual review remain open. |
| Bone-scale squash/blink and green vertex colours have not been verified in Roblox. Blender FBX reimport does not establish target-engine behavior; Studio MCP exposed no connected instance. | Kept exportable clips/vertex colours and an isolated Studio checklist. Blender roundtrips preserved data; no Roblox import or animation playback was observed. | Complete a minimal authorised Studio import, colour, skinning and animation roundtrip before relying on the asset in-game. **Verified:** offline only; Roblox result unresolved. |
| Fusing soft antenna roots required a higher-detail review mesh, close to the per-mesh budget. No device benchmark or performance failure has been measured. | Used voxel fusion/smoothing and decimation within the documented export budget. | Once the visual design is approved, assess topology, mesh count and device performance before producing many creatures. **Verified:** offline counts/export pass; production performance is unverified. |
| A disposable FBX roundtrip renamed the clip `Squash` to `Armature|Armature|Squash` and gave an unrelated camera a copy of the action. | Observed in the 2026-10-31 routing probe on generated primitives; not yet seen on the creature. | Limit FBX export to the creature armature/mesh, disable all-actions baking unless intended, and assert clip names after reimport and in Studio. **Verified:** behaviour reproduced in Blender 5.2.3 only; creature export not re-tested. |

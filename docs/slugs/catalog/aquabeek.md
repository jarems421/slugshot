## Implemented - three visual prototypes, awaiting owner review, 2026-11-10

**Owner request:** "a unique design that still resembles the original thing and has a build similar to Inferno", applying the Inferno and Floppy lessons; three different prototypes to compare. The owner supplied screenshots of the franchise slug's base and transformed forms as study material; they are not stored here and have no reuse rights.

All three use Inferno's accepted body plan (upright, slight forward lean, no arms or feet, pooled base, short tail) as signed-distance concept pages, built from one template by [`build_aquabeek_concepts.py`](../../../tools/slugs/build_aquabeek_concepts.py). | --- | --- | --- |

| B Gillbeak | 8% wider; three gill frills a side, back fin nubs and ripple rings, side-eye with one heavier lid, single tooth | Eel-dragon: short flat bill, ribbon fin, paddle tail |

## Implemented - in the game place, not yet seen in Studio, 2026-11-14

**Owner:** "finalize this as a design, and then build out Aquabeek and the just do the process to get into Roblox and all of that stuff."

**Implemented (offline checks pass; Studio unverified):**
- **Source models:** [`build_aquabeek.py`](../../../tools/slugs/build_aquabeek.py) rebuilds both final forms in Blender from the concept page itself. Instead of a hand port, [`sample_concept.py`](../../../tools/slugs/sample_concept.py) runs the page's own shader in headless Chrome: its distance field on a 0.008 grid (meshed with surface nets, about 250,000 vertices a form), each vertex snapped onto the surface, its colour and glow, and on the transformed form which blade each vertex belongs to. The sways are switched off (`MOTION 0.0`, a new page define that stays 1 on the page), so it is the rest pose. Writes `assets/source/slugs/models/aquabeek/aquabeek-{base,transformed}.blend`. [ADR 0033](../../decisions/0033-concept-shader-sampling.md).
- **Game models:** [`export_aquabeek_roblox.py`](../../../tools/slugs/export_aquabeek_roblox.py) reduces each form to 18,500 triangles (face kept finer), bakes normals and contact shading from the dense mesh, and paints the 2048 colour and glow maps from the shader at each texel's position, so the lashes, irises, lips and scales stay sharp. Game scale 0.43: base 0.96 studs tall, transformed 1.51 long.
  - Base rig: root, body, head, and a weightless `face` bone marking where she faces.
  - Transformed rig: Body, Tail, and two bones along each of the six blades (`Blade{Upper,Middle,Lower}{1,2}.{L,R}`).
  - Checked by reimporting both GLBs (bones, triangles, at most 3 influences) and posing the blades 60 degrees, the tail 30 and the head 20: clean bends, no tearing. Posing only the lower blades moves only them. A first export weighted every blade to the upper bones, because the model was sampled from a page built before `wingFin` reported its blade; the build now refuses a page older than its template.
- **Upload:** [`upload_aquabeek.py`](../../../tools/slugs/upload_aquabeek.py) uploaded both models and their maps as private assets on the owner's account. Ids and SurfaceAppearance templates are only in ignored `private-assets/aquabeek/`.
- **In the game:**
  - `CombatServer` builds `AquabeekIcon`, `AquabeekFlight` and `AquabeekTransformed`, dressed by [`AquabeekAppearance`](../../../experiments/creatures/aquabeek/server/AquabeekAppearance.luau).
  - `FloppyBlaster` puts `AquabeekLoaded` in the canister.
  - `Combat.Slugs.Aquabeek` now transforms like Inferno (prototype numbers unchanged: damage 20, speeds 200/260/320). She was already in the game place's loadout (it sets `SlugshotAbilityLab`), with Crashwave and Mistbank.
  - `TransformationStyles` gives her an aqua glow at 2.6x.
- **Motion:** [`AquabeekMotion`](../../../experiments/creatures/aquabeek/client/AquabeekMotion.client.luau):
  - a slow, graceful idle in the canister (body sway, head tilt and turn);
  - in flight her blades ripple from root to tip, each pair a beat behind the one above, the tail swings and the body rolls;
  - a water trail with small droplets falling away from the tail.
- `verify_combat.py`, `verify_slugs.py` and `verify.py` pass; the game place is rebuilt, and Studio was reopened on it.

The design

Body
A slimmer, upright body with no arms or feet and a slight forward lean, ready to go. It pools onto the floor and ends in a small tail, with sharp blade spines down the back, starting at the smooth back of the head.
Head
A broad, soft slug face with a wide jaw flush with the head. No beak and no brow.
Ear-antennae
Broad at the root like a fox's ear, then shorter and drooping back. They bend and bob along their length, which shows in Live 3D. The tips glow yellow.
Eyes
Wide and burning, with a slight lift at the outer corners, fine rays in the glow and two highlights. One eye has a slightly heavier lid. Cute, but fierce.
Freckles
Seven small red freckles across the nose area, just above the smile.
Mouth
A compact, lopsided smile set into the face. The solid teeth fall into shadow at the corners, with a soft shadow beneath.
Face
Flat cheeks that blend into the head, with faint red marks on them. A deep-red ember crest with a spark either side sits on the forehead, above the eyes.
Belly
A plain orange-to-yellow gradient for now, with no marking.
Colour
Orange skin, deep red stripes running from the spines around the flanks, and a soft yellow underside. No black.
Not updated yet
This is still an early round, with the old brow and snout. It gets redone next, to match the final base form.
Shape
A long, rifled bullet with the same wedge head, and no wings or limbs. The ear-antennae are pressed flat by the speed.
Spin
Spiral plates and three twisted tail fins wrap the body, so you can see him turning.
Flame
Two ribbons of fire corkscrew off the tail, so the spin still shows as a streak when the body is too fast to see.
Colour
Orange at the shoulders running to red at the tail, with glowing yellow grooves between the plates and a furnace-slit mouth.

This is a concept render, not the game model. Glow and flames will need Roblox's own emissive surfaces and particles in-game, and the flight, burst and impact timing still needs testing in Studio.

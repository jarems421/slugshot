Antennae and mouth

Antennae · Feelers
Short, soft feelers in Tazerling's own blue, slim all the way with rounded ends rather than horn points. They bend and whip along their length as he moves (Live 3D). The tips themselves glow electric yellow, white-hot at the very end, and small yellow sparks crawl up each feeler toward the tip, leaping off the skin and back.
Mouth · Grin
The open lopsided grin, about 18% wider and deeper than before, sculpted like Aquabeek's: rolled lips, dimples, a row of top teeth with a fang toward each corner, and a tongue.

The rest of the design

Eyes
Set into the head like Aquabeek's, never bulging, with no lid, set wide apart: sharp, angled almonds whose outer corners sweep up to a point, the top edge a straight line sloping toward the nose. No pupils: each eye burns electric blue, brightest in the middle, with a pale cyan core and faint crackling veins. A glowing gold line runs along the top edge, blue light spills onto the skin, and every couple of seconds a clean spark shoots from an outer corner and fades (Live 3D shows them firing).
Body
Inferno's body plan: upright, a slight forward lean, no arms or feet, pooling onto the floor in uneven lobes, and a short, stubby tail with a sharp yellow spike standing up from its very end, white-hot at the point.
Head
A broad head shaped like Aquabeek's: a wide dome with a rounded crown on a slimmer neck, and a slim muzzle tucked under the eyes so nothing bulges out as cheeks.
Back
Three sharp lightning bolts bulging out of the skin down the spine, biggest at the back of the head: electric yellow, white-hot along their raised middle, with a thin ink edge, on a midnight-indigo back.
Belly
Pale ice blue, following the body's curve, stopping well below the mouth and widening into the base, with zigzag lightning edges, a warm hint of yellow down the middle, and stacked pale-yellow chevrons pointing down it like a charge meter.
Familiar from the original
The royal-blue body and pale belly, yellow lightning on the head and a toothy grin.
Its own
Inferno's body with a round head, glowing lightning eyes, crackling feelers, raised lightning bolts down the back, a chevron charge-meter belly, a spiked tail, and no buck-tooth gap or arms.

A concept render, not the game model. Like Inferno, the agreed version gets rebuilt in Blender from this same maths. Sparks and glow will use Roblox's own beams and emissive surfaces in game. The transformed form follows once the base form is agreed.

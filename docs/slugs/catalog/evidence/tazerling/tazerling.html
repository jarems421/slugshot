Base
Final. Storm cell with a clean body: no lightning on the body; the electricity lives in the eyes, the sparks, the feeler tips and the belly seams.
Transformed
Concept 4. Rammstone's transformed silhouette without the armour, with the reference's electric parts: the head flatter and wider than the base's, roaring wide; a broad chest and one even taper back to a point, no legs. Two big arms growing straight out of the body (no separate shoulder balls), both reaching forward so the hands sit either side of the head; each hand three thick jagged electric fingers with flame-like barbs. The mohawk: three big flames of lightning swept back off the top of the head, the side ones splaying out. The back half electrified: its outline breaks into jagged lightning flames sweeping back, it glows toward the point, which bursts into spikes, and bolts strike and sparks leap off it (Live 3D). Thin yellow lines along the tops of the arms. Lightning trails come later in game.

The rest of the design

Colour
A deep royal blue a shade under round 14's, darkening down the back, with a storm-grey belly.
Antennae
Round 14's feelers: short and soft in the body's blue, bending and whipping as he moves, tips glowing electric yellow with small sparks crawling up them (Live 3D).
Eyes
Under low, subtle lids, each eye glows strongly: deep amber at the rim through electric yellow to a white-hot centre along the middle, breathing gently, with a soft bloom of light round it. Three arcs of lightning strike across it, each on its own clock, snapping on, stuttering, fading and re-striking in a new shape, and the centre flares when one hits (Live 3D). A bold ink line on the top edge.
Sparks
Now and then an arc leaps from an eye's outer corner and crawls back over the head toward the temple, hugging the skin, forking once and crackling into a new shape many times a second while it lives, then fades. Each eye's sparks keep their own clock (Live 3D).
Mouth and belly
The open lopsided grin, now a little higher on the face, and the four belly plates split by glowing lightning seams with the climbing pulse.
Body plan
The round-14 head on Inferno's body: upright, a slight forward lean, pooling onto the floor, a short plain tail.

A concept render, not the game model. Like Inferno, the agreed version gets rebuilt in Blender from this same maths. Sparks and glow will use Roblox's own beams and emissive surfaces in game. The transformed form follows once the base form is agreed.

The design

Body
Inferno's body plan: upright, a slight forward lean, no arms or feet, pooling onto the floor, and a short tail that ends in a small flat fluke.
Fin-ears
Inferno's swept-back ear-antennae rebuilt as webbed fins, with soft scallops between the rays and glowing aqua tips.
Back
One continuous dorsal fin from the tail up over the crown, scalloped between darker rays and paling at the edge. It replaces Inferno's blade spines.
Eyes
Big round eyes with large dark pupils glancing up, two highlights and relaxed teal lids just covering the top. Shallow lenses set into soft sockets, not bug eyes.
Mouth
A wide, open, happy smile with two small rounded teeth at the top and a tongue.
Colour
Ocean-blue front, teal back and crown meeting along a soft wave line, three teal droplet freckles on each cheek.
Belly
A pale sky-blue panel whose top edge rolls like a wave, with a lighter crest along it.
Familiar from the original
Blue and teal two-tone, a pale belly, two fins on the head, big round eyes and a happy open grin.
Its own
Inferno's silhouette and swept fins instead of upright notched fins, a dorsal fin, a wave-edged belly instead of a straight panel, and no buck-tooth gap.
Shape
A marlin-dragon on Inferno's flying body: a long bill, swept pectoral fins with rays and a foam edge, a tall scalloped sail down the back and a crescent tail fin.
Its DNA
The same fin-ears, round eyes (now with a low, determined lid), grin and two-tone colouring.
Wake
A spiralling ribbon of water streams back from the tail, where Inferno has fire.

A concept render, not the game model. Like Inferno, the agreed version gets rebuilt in Blender from this same maths. Glow and water will use Roblox's own emissive surfaces and effects in game.

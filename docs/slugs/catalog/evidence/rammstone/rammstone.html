The design

Body
A tall, upright, sturdy column of a slug with no arms or feet. A smooth tan front, stepped in two V folds down the belly.
Rock armour
Thick, solid wedges of volcanic rock, each ridged down the middle and thickest at its pointed lower edge. They overlap down the back and sides, sweeping down and back, meet in chevrons up the spine to the top of the head, and frame the belly from both sides.
Hood
An angular, faceted rock hood over the front and top of the head, coming down to a sharp V brow over the eyes, with plates breaking out at the temples. A broad charcoal keel runs down the forehead to the point of the brow. A charcoal keel runs down the forehead to the point of the brow.
Horns
Two thick charcoal horns, set wide on the hood, arcing back to sharp points.
Eyes
Slanted, almond-shaped hunter's eyes, cut flat by the brow, in dark stone split by glowing orange lava cracks. The only glow on him.
Mouth
A cocky grin with solid teeth, one corner higher than the other.
Colour
Brick-red rock with fine dark cracks and worn lighter edges, charcoal horns and keel, warm tan skin.

Built from the owner's proposal sheet. This is a concept render, not the game model: the plates, horns and face get rebuilt in Blender from the same shapes once the concept is agreed.

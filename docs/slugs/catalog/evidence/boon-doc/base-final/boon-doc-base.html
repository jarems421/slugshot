The design

Head and body
A crossover of Inferno's and Aquabeek's: every mass halfway between theirs, a broad, fairly flat-topped head wider than a thick neck, on a soft muzzle. Upright with a slight lean, no arms or feet; the pooled base spreads in soft folds like the hem of a robe.
Eye (picked)
Healing mint: pure light with no pupil, a white-gold heart and pale gold turning to soft healing mint toward a teal rim, faint rays turning slowly, a small gleam.
Mouth (picked)
The owner's drawn reference, bigger and taller: a nearly straight upper line lifting at the corners, a shallow curved lower line, one solid white shape (no tooth lines), a bold outline, a flick at his right corner and a short line under the lip.
Wings (picked)
Angel wings after the owner's reference: low on the upper back near the spine, a modest size, set apart so they show past his sides from the front, each leaning off the skin and joined to the body only by a slim ivory stem at its root: a top edge arching up and out, hanging down into long feathers. Sculpted rows of small overlapping covert feathers along the top, long flight feathers below that fan out and curve as they go down, their rounded tips scalloping the bottom edge; each wing bends softly, the lower feathers curling back, with a slow ripple flowing down them (Live 3D). The belly's and eye's warm ivory inner light, glowing softly, warming to gold toward the bottom, with warm creases; swaying a little (Live 3D).
Belly (picked)
Ivory with a fine gold edge, glowing warm from within and warming to luminous white gold toward the base.
Gilded lid
The rolled upper lid over the eye is gold, thickest at the top and tapering to points at the corners, with a fine ink line.
Halo
A gold band with a little thickness, floating just above his head, tilted toward you, glowing and bobbing gently (Live 3D).
Antennae
Soft, of middling length, set toward the edges of the head and flopping back, the tips glowing gold.
Colour
Soft celadon deepening to teal down the back; gold and ivory are the divine light.
Healing light
Small four-point stars of gold light drift up round him (Live 3D).

A concept render, not the game model. Like Inferno, the agreed version gets rebuilt in Blender from this same maths. Glow will use Roblox's own emissive surfaces and effects in game.

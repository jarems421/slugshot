The design

Real volume
The body is built from soft blended shapes, not flat drawings. The lids, cheeks and antennae grow out of the skin, so nothing is pasted on.
Belly
A rounded belly bulges forward under the heavy, slumped head, with a crease beneath the chin. You can see it from the front.
Jelly and slime
Light glows through the thin parts (antennae, tail, edges), the skin has wet sharp highlights, and the body has a gentle, slow wobble.
Ring eyes
Shallow eyes set into the face with heavy upper and lower lids, half-closed and drowsy. Closed rings, not a spiral. Each eye is arranged differently: the more open eye has three bold rings, the droopier eye has four finer ones, and every ring is a different thickness, drifting off-centre. They spin slowly in opposite directions, and the off-centre rings make the spin visible.
Mouth and drool
A small slack, lopsided grin. The drool is part of the mouth: drawn on the skin as a strand hanging straight from the lower lip, outlined by the same dark line as the mouth, like a comic drool.
Foot
A heavy, sagging lower body pools onto the floor in soft, uneven lobes. A slime trail can come later.
Colours
Sage-green body, warm stone belly with soft folds and a muddy green outline, darker sage upper lids, muddy brown-green lower lids, moss-brown marks down the back, and moss-brown antenna tips.
Kept
Concept C's heavy drooping head, subtle cheeks, limp noodle antennae, dark tired patches under the eyes and a short tail.

This is a live concept render, not the game model. Roblox can't show light glowing through skin, so in-game the jelly look will come from the shape, wet highlights in the texture and the wobble animation.

# First slug: Flopper-inspired creature

## Confirmed — owner brief, 2026-10-31

Create a distinct original Slugshot creature inspired by Flopper. A soft, squishy and somewhat goofy personality; pale cream/light yellow-green body, olive facial accents, large expressive eyes, a broad head, narrower flexible middle, flared lower body and small flexible appendages. Squash/stretch and expressive motion are central. This species does not transform. This is the first creature design; the reference is not a mandate to import or reproduce someone else's model.

The owner supplied two screenshots as visual study material: `Screenshot 2026-10-31 005433.png` and `Screenshot 2026-10-31 005444.png`. They show a pale upright creature with green eye regions, a green belly, small arms and two long flexible projections. Those screenshots are third-party visual references, not textures, model geometry or assets supplied with reuse rights; they remain outside this public repository.

## Confirmed — owner revision, 2026-10-31

The owner rejected the first draft as blocky and hard, with an oversized head, protruding eyes and unwanted feet. The revised direction is an original-looking, much softer slug with a more proportionate head; hypnotic spiral eyes that sit on the face; a white base with vibrant, irregular, softly diffused green sickness/bacteria-like markings on **all sides**; and a **very small tail**, not feet or a long tail. A squishy appearance is important. The earlier green belly patch, detached eyes and foot-like lobes are rejected.

**Latest refinement:** the owner finds the bright white too clean and the upright body too hard. Use a slightly dirty, subtly warm off-white, staying short of cream/yellow. The lower body should relax and spread fully onto the ground. The short tail should be a low, soft triangular continuation of the body, not a separate thin tail or rounded nub. Keep the original-inspired general slug shape while developing its own design.

**Subsequent colour/antenna direction:** the owner asked for a stronger cream/dirty-yellow shift and reinstated antennae that grow out of the slug and flop down. They then rejected the guessed colour and instructed us to match the original supplied Flopper image. The current base uses the pale cream sampled from the central head region of `Screenshot 2026-10-31 005433.png`: approximately sRGB `(228, 222, 184)`, converted to linear `(0.7758, 0.7305, 0.4793)` for Blender. This is a reference-colour sample, not a calibrated material measurement. Studio preview lighting/backdrop is neutral gray to avoid the earlier green cast. The earlier off-white and guessed brown/yellow directions are superseded by this reference match; spiral eyes and dispersed green markings remain.

**Latest antenna feedback:** the owner still finds the antennae stiff and too obviously attached, and requests a small forward turn rather than a sideways sweep. The new curves lean forward, taper more above the roots and droop lower. Roots begin deeper inside the head and receive extra local smoothing; animation weights now blend along more of the antenna length so the idle flexes them more gradually. Colour remains unchanged from the sampled reference.

**Latest marking/silhouette feedback:** darken and mute the green markings so they look slightly muddy. Widen the head a little above the middle, creating a subtle hourglass-like contour without exaggerated proportions. The current stain colour is muted olive `(0.16, 0.34, 0.075)` in linear RGB. The upper profile is rounder and wider than the middle while the relaxed lower base remains widest. Cream skin, forward-drooping antennae, surface eyes and the short integrated tail remain the current direction.

**Latest head/softness feedback:** the owner likes the general direction but wants depth in side view, a small forehead and a squishier head/sides/perimeter. The revised upper profile has a rounded forward forehead and deeper cheeks/crown, with broad, gentle settling asymmetry through the flanks. The Blender material has softer light scattering and the idle gently deforms the head volume as well as breathing through the body. Antenna tips were given extra clearance after the fuller forehead initially touched one tip. Palette and the gentle head/waist contour are retained. Subjective softness remains for owner review.

**Image-right tip correction:** the owner still saw the right antenna touching the forehead. That iteration moved the antenna on the viewer's right in the front render (`feelers_left` in model coordinates) farther out and forward. Dedicated geometry checks required outboard and forward clearance from the forehead, in addition to the existing connected-skin/topology checks. This produced a mismatch in the two antenna shapes, addressed in the next revision.

**Symmetry follow-up:** the owner asked for both antennae to match the more inward shape, while keeping them clear of the forehead. One mirrored pair of inward curves now defines the rest shape. Tip symmetry and clearance are checked in the rest pose and clearance is sampled through the idle. Visual approval remains open. The previous unequal render and other lessons are preserved in [Blender lessons](../blender-lessons.md).

## Confirmed — Floppy, final design, 2026-10-31

The owner confirmed this as **the Floppy design** ("this is perfect... this is the Floppy design. This is our Floppy"), after the colour, belly and eyelid touch-ups. It replaces the earlier V1 save. The previous concept rounds and the rejected Blender silhouette variant were deleted at the owner's request. The design is a rotatable real-time concept, not yet the game model: [V1 source](evidence/floppy/floppy.html) (open in a browser) with [front](evidence/floppy/front.png), [three-quarter](evidence/floppy/three-quarter.png), [side](evidence/floppy/side.png), [back](evidence/floppy/back.png) and [face](evidence/floppy/face.png) renders.

- **Personality:** weak, drowsy, slightly drunk/stoned.
- **Body:** concept C "Slumped Sleeper". The heavy, taller head has a rounded dome and droops slightly forward over a narrower torso. A rounded belly bulges under the chin, the cheeks are subtle, and the tail is short. The lower body is heavy and sags asymmetrically, pooling onto the floor in soft uneven lobes; there is no flat plinth.
- **Construction:** soft blended volumes (signed-distance smooth unions), so the lids, cheeks and antennae grow out of the skin.
- **Eyes:** shallow lens-shaped eyes set wide apart, with soft edges blending into the face. Heavy, half-closed upper lids are uneven (the character's left droops more), and there are lower lids. Each eye has closed concentric rings, not a spiral, arranged off-centre and spinning slowly in opposite directions. The more open eye (character's right) has three bold rings of different thickness; the other has four finer rings, each a different thickness.
- **Eye bags:** a mauve-brown crescent of darker skin under each lower lid, fading softly onto the lid and into the skin.
- **Mouth:** a small, slack, lopsided grin. The drool is part of the mouth: a pale strand hanging from the lower lip slightly in from the low corner, outlined by the mouth's dark line, in comic style.
- **Antennae:** each is one smooth, tapering curve rooted on the forehead that arcs out and flops down to a soft bulb.
- **Colour and surface (2026-10-31):** the owner first supplied a blue-green palette (`#91B8AE` body, `#8996AC` upper lids, `#31585B` lower lids, `#D8D3BE` belly, `#527F79` back markings), then found it "way too blue" and asked for green/brown. Current colours: yellow-brown sage body `#B0B48A`, warm stone belly `#D8D3BE` with soft geometric belly folds: full rolls of skin that bulge out and tuck under the roll below, with a shadowed underside; slightly uneven; curving up at the sides enclosed by a soft muddy brown-green outline `#6E6B3E` (painted brown stripes and a brown outline were tried and rejected), upper lids darker than the skin but lighter than the lower lids `#888F68`, muddy brown-green lower lids `#5A5934`, moss-brown back markings and antenna-tip bulbs `#6A6B45`. Antenna stripes were tried and replaced by coloured tip bulbs. Back pattern: five soft rounded lozenges down the spine, shrinking toward the tail, with light shading on the crown and back. The concept skin is glossy and wet with a warm glow through thin parts, which Roblox can't reproduce. Freckles are off.
- **Motion:** a slow, gentle wobble with the head barely swaying, antenna drift and spinning rings.
- **Considering (not approved):** a slime trail, final decorative markings and other touch-up details.
- **Blender build (Implemented/verified offline, 2026-10-31):** the owner asked for Floppy "one-to-one exactly with the animation and all" in Blender. [`tools/slugs/build_floppy.py`](../../../tools/slugs/build_floppy.py) ports the concept's shape and colour functions to NumPy and writes [`floppy.blend`](../../../assets/source/slugs/models/floppy/floppy.blend) plus [textures](../../../assets/source/slugs/models/floppy/textures/). The steps:
  - samples the skin at 6.5 mm, meshes it with surface nets, decimates to 360,000 triangles and re-projects every vertex onto the exact surface;
  - bakes a 2048 body texture by colouring each texel from its exact rest position;
  - builds lens eyes whose ring textures come from the same pattern function;
  - adds an eight-bone rig (root, lower/upper body, head, two antennae, two eyes) and a 12 s looping `Idle` at 24 fps covering breath, travelling wobble, head loll, antenna drift and ring spin.
  To make the loop seamless, the concept's motion rates were rounded to whole cycles in 12 s, and the concept page was updated to the same rates. Bones approximate the concept's continuous wobble, which can't be matched exactly. **Verified in Blender 4.5:** Cycles renders from five angles ([front](evidence/floppy/blender/front.png), [three-quarter](evidence/floppy/blender/three-quarter.png), [side](evidence/floppy/blender/side.png), [back](evidence/floppy/blender/back.png), [face](evidence/floppy/blender/face.png)); the idle loop seam is identical; the base stays on the floor at sampled frames; maximum idle displacement is 9.5 cm, at the antenna tips. **Unresolved:** owner check in Blender; a game-budget version (360k triangles and a 2048 texture are far above Roblox limits); FBX export; Studio import and playback. Wet glow (scattering) does not carry to Roblox.
- **Colour depth (2026-10-31):** the owner called the build "perfect" but too light/pale and asked for more depth. The body texture now multiplies in baked ambient occlusion (folds, lids, creases, antenna roots), uses a slightly richer and deeper grade, and the Blender material has less scattering and sheen.
- **Roblox version (Implemented/verified offline, 2026-10-31):** [`tools/slugs/export_floppy_roblox.py`](../../../tools/slugs/export_floppy_roblox.py) writes ignored `build/slugs/floppy/roblox/`. Contents: an 8,000-triangle skinned body and two 400-triangle eyes; 1024 colour and normal maps baked from the full-detail source; at most 3 influences per vertex; 8 bones; the model facing +Y so the FBX gives Roblox -Z; 1 unit = 1 stud (about 2.5 studs tall). The idle is a 12 s clip with breathing converted from bone scale to small translations, because Roblox bones are CFrame-only. **Verified offline by FBX reimport:** bones, influences, normalised weights, facing, 12.0 s duration, loop seam 1e-6, zero bone scale and real motion; renders: [game three-quarter](evidence/floppy/blender/game-three-quarter.png), [game face](evidence/floppy/blender/game-face.png). **Unresolved:** Studio import, SurfaceAppearance, animation playback and device frame rate (owner step, see [Studio import](../studio-import.md#floppy-import-owner-step)); nothing uploaded.
- **Game size and gloss (Confirmed, 2026-10-31):** the owner found the first Roblox Floppy "way too big": slugs must fit in the blasters. The blaster is 2.2 studs long, with a ~0.22-stud canister and a ~0.24-stud muzzle channel. The export now uses a 0.09 scale: 0.187 × 0.201 × 0.222 studs, and the export fails if it doesn't fit. The owner also asked for a subtle slimy gloss: the GLB now carries roughness maps (skin 0.22, eyes 0.08) for Roblox SurfaceAppearance. The test place shows Floppy on a pedestal beside the blaster.
- **Name:** the owner names this slug **Floppy** (Confirmed by the owner). Rights note: it is close to the franchise name "Flopper"; a name/IP check before any publication remains Unresolved.

## Reference research and rights

The community-maintained [SlugTerra Wiki Flopper entry](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Flopper) calls Flopper a common, powerless, unknown-element species and describes stretching, slippery contact and smoke-related uses. Species number 000 is wiki classification; the owner supplied the names Noodle, Dizzy and Old Timer and the stated debut. These are observations of the franchise reference, not confirmed Slugshot canon. A search-indexed copy of the page was inspected on 2026-10-31; direct access was blocked by the site's robots policy. Franchise characters and names belong to their rights holders.

Reference facts supplied by the owner: Floppers do not develop a transformed form, tend to be sleepy/stretchy/clumsy, and can flatten on impact. The wiki describes stretching, slippery contact, a smoke trail, and a highly situational blaster backfire trick. The owner wants a creature with more potential for clever use in Slugshot. These examples are research and proposals, not implemented game rules.

The owner suggested an animated Sketchfab model as the main 3D study reference. [This Slugterra collection](https://sketchfab.com/DanSword/collections/slugterra-4da93539bd6d42a0b50e0942048a9a8f) lists an animated “Slug Flopper”, but the collection page returned HTTP 403 in this environment. Its actual animation, author and license could not be verified. No Sketchfab model was downloaded, executed, copied or imported. The supplied screenshots provide the directly inspected form references; do not claim an animation study occurred.

## Considering — Slugshot gameplay ideas, not implemented

A weak slippery patch, small smoke puff, a carefully placed tripwire or a squash/splat at impact could reward inventive use. The owner described these as ideas; exact effects, balance, damage and legality in a future game remain open. The franchise-specific “Stab Fire” example is a reference note, not an approved Slugshot mechanic. No projectile, collision, combat or abilities are implemented by this design task.

## Implemented/verified

**Revised draft, awaiting owner review:** a pale cream body with a softly rounded forehead, fuller head depth, a gentle waist and a spread grounded base. Broad settling curves soften the sides. Two mirrored antennae hang forward, with roots smoothed into the same connected skin as the head and short triangular tail, and tips clear of the fuller forehead. Conformal green spiral eyes, the small downturned mouth and diffuse muddy olive markings remain. It has no foot meshes, separate tail nub or belly patch. The body material uses restrained highlights and subsurface scattering in Blender. The working identifier `flopper-inspired` is not an approved final species name.

- Editable [Blender source](../../../assets/source/slugs/models/flopper-inspired/protoform.blend), reproducible [authoring script](../../../tools/slugs/create_flopper_inspired.py), [species configuration](../../../assets/source/slugs/species/flopper-inspired.json) and [10-bone rig definition](../../../assets/source/slugs/rigs/flopper-inspired-v1.json). Created as original procedural geometry by Codex for the owner's 2026-10-31 request; no imported mesh, texture or animation. The reference screenshots and the Blender source are kept separate.
- The authored rig has body, head, jaw, tail, independently weighted eyes and left/right feeler bones. The antenna roots blend head and feeler weights; the idle gently sways the antennae and adds subtle volume-preserving head deformation. It provides Idle (2 s), Blink, Hop, Launch and Impact (1 s each) actions. The asymmetrical blink and nonuniform squash use bone-scale animation in Blender; Roblox support is not established.
- Generated outputs: `build/slugs/flopper-inspired/exports/protoform/model.fbx` and five individual animation FBXs; `build/slugs/flopper-inspired/previews/index.html` contains front/side/three-quarter stills, a 360° turntable, and each motion sequence at its authored duration. [Review portrait](evidence/flopper-inspired-portrait.png), [front](evidence/flopper-inspired-front.png), [side](evidence/flopper-inspired-side.png), and [rear](evidence/flopper-inspired-rear.png). These images show the generated 3D model, not a 2D concept.
- **Verified offline:** Blender 4.5.14 LTS headless generation, authored source validation, export/reimport of the model and five clips, dimensions/bone hierarchy/normalized weights, nonzero motion, idle loop seam, preview generation, 23 configuration tests, strict Luau and isolated builds. Targeted checks confirm head depth greater than waist depth, a forward forehead, gentle head/waist width proportions and the base remaining widest. The skin is connected and closed without antenna handles; tips hang down/forward and remain free of the forehead. Checks also cover marking coverage around all four quadrants, no feet, a short low tail, eye surfaces within 0.016 units of the head and FBX vertex colours. Over 100 underside vertices form a flat contact patch at least 1.3 units wide and 0.9 deep; those vertices stay within 0.003 units of the floor during sampled idle and impact-squash poses. Head deformation is measured in the idle. This is authored geometry/animation, not a simulated soft-body system. The current detailed review model has 22,412 triangles across six mesh objects and 10 bones, with 18,000 triangles in the connected skin. The per-mesh budget is 20,000, total budget 40,000; production optimisation and device testing remain pending. Exact source/FBX hashes, exporter settings and clip measurements are preserved in [export evidence](evidence/flopper-inspired-export.json).
- Run `python tools/verify_slugs.py --blender` with `BLENDER` set to the existing executable to rebuild and validate both species. To rebuild just this design from the saved source, run `python tools/slugs/cli.py all --species flopper-inspired`; render portraits with `blender --background --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/slugs/render_flopper_portrait.py`. The authoring script deliberately overwrites the editable `.blend` if run again; preserve manual edits first.

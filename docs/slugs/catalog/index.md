## Confirmed future roster bank - 2026-11-04

The owner wants **14 slugs total**, including Floppy and Inferno, with a possible 12-slug launch if production takes longer. The 12 additional choices are **Rammstone, Tazerling, Frostcrawler, Aquabeek, Arachnet, Boon Doc, Tormato, Vinedrill, Phosphoro, Flatulorhinkus, Hop Rock and Frightgeist**. Their visual designs remain unresolved; Aquabeek's and Arachnet's ability cores are selected above; other additional kits and Arachnet's movement-passive details remain open. These names identify the reference species discussed, not completed Slugshot assets.

Read [roster and ability planning](../ability-planning.md) before proposing or building an ability. It records the owner's cross-slug interaction direction, naming context and examples. **Examples are inspiration, not approved kits: propose alternatives and choose a better fit where available.** Preserve the owner's confirmed Inferno cores and **Aquabeek's finalized Crashwave/Mistbank pair**. Preserve Arachnet's confirmed Snapbind/Silkline cores; detailed rules remain open. Frostcrawler's jagged Ice Wall passive is confirmed; Iceburst, Brittle and Frostguard mains remain unselected; second-pass gimmicks were rejected.

## Ability rule

**Current clarification, owner, 2026-11-04:** each slug needs a **player-hit effect** and a **passive ability triggered by an environmental hit**, including **floors, walls and ceilings**. The shot's hit target chooses the outcome automatically. This expands the older one-ability wording below; there is still no pre-shot ability selection. Inferno keeps Backdraft on player hits and has fire as its surface-effect direction. Exact surface rules and other species' kits remain open. See [player-hit/passive planning](../ability-planning.md#confirmed---player-hit-effect-and-passive-surface-ability).

**Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-02:** each slug has **one ability**, with no choosing between abilities before a shot. Each ability should be **versatile**, rewarding how creatively it's used rather than doing one fixed thing. The owner's example, an illustration and not a strict template: an electric slug electrocutes a player it hits, but fired into a pool of water it electrifies the water, slowing players over a larger area for slightly less damage. Abilities are made in-house and inspired by Slugterra's elements, not copied from its named moves.

**Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-04:** abilities must also support intelligent sequential chains and elemental/positional interactions across the roster. Twin-blast fusion is a separate future idea. Pool electricity, water orbs/grouping, destructible rubble and gas plus knockback remain examples to compare against other proposals, not selected abilities. See the [current planning note](../ability-planning.md#considering---examples-for-inspiration-not-selected-abilities).

## New slug records

Copy the [new slug record template](template.md) for each future creature. Record:

1. **Confirmed:** owner instructions, distinguishing silhouette, palette, anatomy, personality and transformation behavior.
2. **Reference research:** links, author and license where known, access limitations and what was actually inspected. Reference images and other artists' models are not automatically reusable assets.
3. **Considering:** possible abilities, effects, rarities and gameplay role; do not treat ideas as approved mechanics.
4. **Implemented/verified:** own source and export paths, creator/provenance, tool version, rig ID, action names, generation settings and checks.
5. **Unresolved:** owner feedback, visual quality, import/runtime evidence, licensing or gameplay decisions.

Keep draft source and generated exports separately, as described in the [species guide](../species-guide.md). Do not publish or upload an asset solely because it appears in this catalogue.

[Per-species inspect briefs](inspect-profiles.md) record the owner-requested Floppy and Inferno personality gestures separately from their combat abilities.

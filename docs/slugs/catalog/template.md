# Slug design record: <working name>

## Confirmed — owner direction

Record the request date, intended silhouette, body proportions, colour/pattern, eyes and face, appendages, personality, and whether the creature has a transformed form. Keep working names and approval state explicit. Link any owner-supplied reference images and describe what they show without assuming permission to redistribute them.

## Reference research and rights

Record direct source links, source type, author/licence where known, dates inspected and any blocked or inaccessible material. Clearly distinguish franchise lore, user descriptions and inferences from Slugshot design decisions. Do not import other creators' models, animations, screenshots or textures without verified rights.

## Considering — gameplay and animation ideas

Record proposed abilities, collection/inventory concepts, motion and effects. Mark them as ideas until the owner approves an implementation; a visual draft does not implement combat rules.

## Implemented/verified

Link editable source, generation script, configuration, rigs, exported models/clips, previews, provenance and checks. Record tools/versions, units/axes, rest skeleton, actions/durations, triangle/mesh counts, any verified imports and actual Studio evidence. Say exactly what was run, not what was merely built or type-checked.

## Unresolved

Record owner feedback, final design/name, rights questions, visual and deformation quality, performance, import/permissions and unimplemented gameplay. Link the [Studio checklist](../studio-import.md) and ensure no uploads occur without authorization.

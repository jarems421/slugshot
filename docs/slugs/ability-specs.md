# Ability specs

The detailed pass on every confirmed ability. Each concept is picked; this note defines exactly how it works.

**Owner, 2026-11-05:** "we're going to go through each main ability, each passive ability, and go through like the specifics, how they work, what counters it, like go through the elements, like, oh, how water combats fire, what combats fire, what combats water. All of that type of stuff, rarities for different slugs, classes ... But mainly, because obviously that classes stuff and whatnot is pretty basic to do, but mainly we're going to go into the details of each ability. So we know, how they work and define them clearly."

Picks and the job map stay in [ability planning](ability-planning.md); each slug's history stays in its catalogue record. A spec here is Considering until the owner approves it, then Confirmed.

## Order

1. **Element chart:** what clears, beats, reduces, boosts or is immune, [below](#element-chart---confirmed-2026-11-05). It changes every spec, so it comes first. It replaces the earlier [interaction table](ability-planning.md#confirmed---abilities-interact-2026-11-05) once approved.
2. **Each slug's main and passive,** one slug at a time, using the template below.
3. **Classes and rarities,** last.

## Element chart - confirmed, 2026-11-05

**Owner:** "let's separate the element chart. Into what is beaten by what is cleared by what beats what it boosts. And then what reduces the effect of it, but doesn't fully clear it. So we can have a clear element chart. And then what's just immune to everything, because for example, something like fright or something isn't really" (message cut off).

**Owner, 2026-11-05:** "for every boost clearance amplification You need to have justification based on the abilities. On how this works ... And why it boosts, why it amplifies, why it does whatever." So every row names the abilities involved, what happens in a match and why. A rule with no real ability case is dropped. **Owner, 2026-11-05:** "finalize everything as confirmed ... So finalize all the proposals as confirmed. And that'll be our element. Chart." Every row below is Confirmed.

| Kind | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Clears | Removes the effect or object completely |
| Beats | Wins head-on: destroys or cancels the other's object, or stops it working |
| Reduces | Weakens or shortens it without removing it |
| Boosts | Makes it stronger, bigger or longer |
| Sets off | Triggers it early |
| Immune | No element affects it; the counter is playing smart |

### Reaction kit

**Confirmed, 2026-11-05.** Interactions reuse the abilities' own effects plus six shared reactions, so animation is planned as six effects rather than one per rule. Mostly particles, not character animation.

| Reaction | Used by |
| --- | --- |
| Steam puff | Crashwave putting out fire; Mistbank weakening Fire Curtain; Iceburst shortening burns |
| Ignite burst | Fire, Sticky Bomb or Mine igniting Stink Cloud |
| Break | Rammstone and explosions breaking Ice Wall and rock cover; fire melting Ice Wall (drips instead of chunks) |
| Burn away | Fire burning Bramble and Silkline |
| Cleanse | Mending Shot and Healing Spring clearing or shortening statuses |
| Status markers | Wet, burning, bound, tangled and sick on players |

Tweaks to existing effects: Bramble scales up when watered; Tornado's particles turn orange or green when carrying fire or gas; Mistbank tints and sparks when electrified; healing sparkle greys out in Stink Cloud; an arc-triggered Mine plays its normal explosion early. Mistbank parting under wind can start as the mist fading where the wind passes.

**Building it:** one shared system of tags on objects (fire, gas, flammable, cover with health, plant) and statuses on players (burning, bound, tangled, sick, wet), with these rules as one table. Suggested order: status and health rules first, then overlap rules, then partial removal (gaps) and carrying.

### Teammates and enemies

- **Slugs pass through teammates, confirmed 2026-11-06:** "slug should phase through, only healing type slugs etc should be able to hit team mates." Only healing slugs, such as Boon Doc, can hit a teammate.

**Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-05:** "your interaction shouldn't affect your own team's abilities in that way. I feel like you should be able to affect your teammates' abilities in terms of amplification. But not like destroying them. So like, let's say you shoot electric at someone's water, then yeah, it'll amplify that water. If it's your teammates."

- **No friendly harm, confirmed 2026-11-05:** "you shouldn't be able to damage your teammates or negatively affect your teammates." On a teammate's ability only boosts apply. Clears, beats, reduces and sets off do nothing to your own team's objects: your Fire Curtain never burns a teammate's Bramble, and your Livewire never sets off a teammate's Mine.
- **On an enemy's ability:** every rule applies.
- **An enemy boosting your ability, confirmed 2026-11-05:** the boost belongs to whoever adds it. Owner: "if you let's say you shoot a tornado at someone, and then an enemy inflames it with [Inferno's] passive, then if you have a teammate inside of the tornado, or walking through the tornado or something, then they'll get burned. Obviously, your tornado shouldn't drag in other people in your team. But let's say you walk through the tornado, then you'll get burnt. Or let's say someone electrifies [Mistbank] ... your teammates will get electrified."
  - An enemy's Fire Curtain sets your Tornado alight: your teammates walking through it are burned. Your Tornado still never drags your own team.
  - An enemy's electricity in your Mistbank: your teammates inside are electrified.

### Crashwave (main, cooldown 6 s)

| Field | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| One sentence | The hit damages the target, then a wave carries on along the shot's line and hits enemies behind them |
| Trigger | Main armed (Q), then a direct hit on an enemy player. A miss wastes it. Slugs pass through teammates |
| Direct hit | Full slug damage to the player hit |
| The wave | Starts at the target and travels about **25 studs** along the shot's line. About **6 studs** wide. Faster than running, so you sidestep or jump it rather than outrun it. Deals about **a third** of the direct hit to each enemy it touches, once each |
| Airborne target | The water carries on through the air and splashes down further along, then rolls from there |
| Terrain | Climbs anything a player could walk on, including stairs; stops at walls and ledges. No knockback |
| Cover | Ice Wall or rock cover it reaches takes heavy damage but is not destroyed, and the wave stops there. People behind the cover are safe from it. Hitting the cover directly with the main armed is a miss |
| Soaked | Everyone it touches is soaked for **4 s** |
| Teammates | The slug passes through teammates. The wave never hurts them, and a burning teammate it passes is put out |
| Overlap | A target is damaged once per wave; two waves hit separately |
| Counters | Sidestep or jump the wave; stand behind cover; don't stand in line behind the person being shot |
| Elements | Clears Fire Curtain where it passes and ends burns; damages Ice Wall and rock cover; soaked players take farther Chain Lightning arcs; grows Bramble it rolls over |
| Building it | A hitbox moving along the shot's line, following walkable ground with raycasts, ending at walls; a list of who it already hit |

### Mistbank (passive, cooldown 4 s, up to 2 at once)

| Field | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| One sentence | Hitting a surface leaves a thick mist that nobody can see through |
| Floor | A dome about **14 studs** across and taller than a player, so it hides people standing in it |
| Wall | The same size, attached to the wall where it hit. It does not stretch down to the floor or up to the ceiling |
| Ceiling | A hanging bubble under the ceiling. It does not reach the floor |
| Timing | Billows out to full size over about **0.5 s**, lasts **8 s**, then fades. It stays where it formed |
| Inside | Murky: you see about **4 studs**. Name tags and health bars inside are hidden from outside |
| Shots | Does not block shots and deals no damage |
| Soaked | Anyone inside is soaked while inside and for **2 s** after leaving |
| Cap | Two per player; a third replaces your oldest |
| Counters | Flank around it, wait 8 s, fire in blind, or blow a gap with Tornado or Updraft |
| Elements | Weakens Fire Curtain inside it and shortens burns; Livewire inside it electrifies the whole mist; Chain Lightning cannot arc into it; grows Bramble inside it; Lantern still reveals anyone inside who looks at the light |
| Building it | A particle cloud plus an invisible region for the checks; name tags hidden on the client |

## How abilities activate

**Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-06 - arm with a key:** "do the q and e thing, but make it do if u double tap them or smth u can deselect so u just do a normal slug shot". This replaces the earlier assumption that what a shot hits decides main or passive.

- **Q arms the main, E arms the passive** for the next shot. Double-tap the key, or similar, to disarm and fire a normal slug. Unarmed shots are plain slugs.
- **Armed main:** works on a direct enemy hit. **A miss wastes it and starts the cooldown.**
- **Armed passive:** forms where the slug lands. On a surface, as each spec describes. **On a player, it forms where they stand:** Mistbank around them, Livewire, Bramble, Stink Cloud or a Mine under them. Ice Wall and rock cover grow at the floor where they stand, shoving them aside. Silkline pulls the player toward you.
- **Cooldowns start when the armed shot is fired.**

**Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-05:** "let's do a mix of 1 and 2. But let's not do a strict one limit. Like, we can do maybe you could have 2 [Mistbanks] active at once. Etc. And it can change for each ability depending on how it would affect the game." Concern behind it: full charge is quick, so "people could just put like 20 mist bombs everywhere".

- **Separate cooldowns for main and passive.** Owner: passives shorter, mains "a little longer". People won't wait long, so values are short.
- **A cap on active passive objects, set per ability.** Placing one more replaces your oldest.
- **Uses per match or per life:** not limited for now; decide in playtesting (owner, 2026-11-06).

**Considering - assistant's suggestions, 2026-11-05:**

- **Per ability, not one general cooldown.** Each slug's main and passive have their own, so balance can be set ability by ability. They keep ticking while the slug is holstered and show on its loadout slot.

## Shared combat rules, 2026-11-06

From an outside review the owner shared, checked against these specs and the code, then the owner's choices. Durations and numbers are starting values for playtesting.

### Control effects

**Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-06:** "there should just be a slight cooldown before it can affect you again. So maybe we'll do like a 2-second cooldown. That gives, or 3-second cooldown, which would give you enough time to hide to return behind cover. But not just not working again." This replaces the assistant's split into lockdown, vision and weaken groups.

- **Control effects:** Snapbind, Tangle, Flash, Jump Scare and Nausea.
- **When one ends, you are immune to all of them for 3 s** (2 s is the alternative to try in playtesting), long enough to get back behind cover. Then they work on you again as normal. This also covers Snapbind's own re-bind immunity.
- Slows and forced movement are not control effects; they have their own rules below.

| Type | Abilities | Rule | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slows | Iceburst (50%), Bramble (60%), Tornado (half speed) | Only the strongest applies; they never add up. Nobody goes below **50%** speed | Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-06 |
| Forced movement | Tornado pull, Silkline pull, Shove Punch, Sticky Bomb push, Updraft | One at a time; the newest replaces the old. Big knockbacks (Shove Punch, Sticky Bomb, Updraft) end a Snapbind and move the player. Pulls (Tornado, Silkline) cannot move a bound player. Example: Shove Punch hitting someone mid-Silkline ends the pull and knocks them back | Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-06 |

### Transformation and arming

- **Every shot transforms in flight**, except slugs that never transform, such as Floppy. Arming adds the ability; it does not decide the transformation. Nausea is the exception: sick players' slugs do not transform. **Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-06:** Nausea stays half damage, half speed and no transform for 3.5 s.
- Charge mainly changes speed and accuracy. Only abilities that say so scale with charge, such as Shove Punch. Ranges stay as specified.
- Armed slugs show a small glow that enemies can see up close, and the HUD confirms it for you.
- Switching slug, unequipping or dying cancels the arming. Arming costs nothing; the cooldown starts when the armed shot fires.
- **Keys, confirmed 2026-11-06:** Q arms the main, E arms the passive, and **Inventory moves from E to F**. Phones and controllers get their own buttons calling the same actions. The code change waits until abilities are built ([KeybindDefinitions.luau](../../experiments/movement/shared/KeybindDefinitions.luau) still has Inventory on E).

### Placed objects and Decoy

- A Mine, Livewire, Lantern or other object disappears when the surface under it disappears, such as a melting Ice Wall or a fallen spire.
- Every object is cleaned up when it expires, is destroyed, its owner leaves, or the match ends.
- Objects keep their element's colour, with a team-coloured marker or outline.
- Each ability keeps its own cap, plus a team-wide budget for objects, tuned in playtests.
- **Decoy, confirmed 2026-11-06:** counts as an enemy for Chain Lightning arcs, Mine triggers and armed abilities, which are spent on it. It never counts for kills, healing or objectives.

### Element additions

| Abilities | What happens | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crashwave and Stink Cloud | No interaction | Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-06 |
| Explosions and Mistbank | No interaction; blasts do not clear or fade the mist | Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-06 |
| Tornado and Silkline | No interaction | Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-06 |
| Livewire on Ice Wall | Works as a patch on that surface; the wall does not conduct | Confirmed, owner, 2026-11-06 |

### Match rules

- **Health regeneration, confirmed 2026-11-06:** **2 HP per second** after **6 s** without taking damage. Health is 100.
- Kill credit goes to whoever caused the lethal damage. In a chain reaction, whoever set it off gets the kill and the others an assist. For example, igniting someone's Stink Cloud gives the igniter the kill and the cloud's owner an assist.
- A team can have duplicate slugs, including two Boon Docs. Identical healing does not stack.
- Five loadout slots, one of each species per player.
- Fusion, classes and rarities come later. Rarity never changes damage or health.

# Vision

## Confirmed

On 2026-11-04 the owner clarified the [two impact outcomes](slugs/ability-planning.md#confirmed---player-hit-effect-and-passive-surface-ability): a player-hit effect and a **passive ability** triggered by hitting environmental surfaces, including floors, walls and ceilings. "Passive" names this surface-triggered effect; it is not an always-on effect or a second selected firing mode. Inferno retains Backdraft on player hits, with surface fire as its environmental direction; exact surface rules remain open.

On 2026-11-04 the owner selected a [14-slug roster bank and cross-slug ability direction](slugs/ability-planning.md), with 12 acceptable at launch if production takes longer. Abilities should support sequential tactical chains and elemental, positional and terrain interactions. Preserve Inferno's confirmed Backdraft/surface-fire cores and **[Aquabeek's final Crashwave/Mistbank pair](slugs/catalog/aquabeek.md)**. [Arachnet](slugs/catalog/arachnet.md) has confirmed Snapbind main and Silkline pull/swing passive; detailed rules and unselected interaction examples remain provisional. Frostcrawler's [jagged Ice Wall cover passive](slugs/catalog/frostcrawler.md) is selected; its main remains open after the initial ideas were rejected. Ability distinctions should come from useful, readable roles rather than novelty for its own sake; avoid duplicating Arachnet’s movement bind across the roster. Other kits, numerical balance and implementation remain unresolved.

On 2026-11-01 the owner approved local post-shot recovery: slugs land, shake off and burrow into the ground, representing a return to inventory. Floppy belly-flops, wriggles and rolls onto its back, then hops into its burrow. Recovery presentation is independent of firing cooldown readiness, avoiding fast return traffic across crowded matches. Floppy's sequence is implemented with offline and limited Studio verification; owner animation approval and future species clips remain open. See [recovery](slugs/recovery.md).

On 2026-10-31 the owner requested a first, original Flopper-inspired small slug with a soft expressive design and no transformation, plus a lasting catalogue for all future slug briefs and research. Potential abilities remain ideas; no combat implementation was requested. See [design record](slugs/catalog/flopper-inspired.md).

The owner additionally authorized a reusable custom-rig creature **technical pipeline** on 2026-10-30, with temporary test creatures in an isolated worktree. That request did not by itself approve finished ProtoformBase art, species or combat; the first original creature draft was requested separately above. See [ADR 0016](decisions/0016-slug-asset-pipeline.md) and [pipeline](slugs/pipeline.md). Concurrent map and movement work stays separate.

Slugshot is an original Roblox multiplayer creature-combat project. The verified development foundation is preserved. The owner authorizes isolated movement research, a playable sandbox, relevant free resource evaluation and the original Rocklock cavern greybox (2026-10-30). The later spaciousness feedback governs the map iteration; final environment/combat design is still open. Use original or appropriately licensed IP. Prototype mechanics, controls and camera parameters are not permanent design decisions; final combat/creature/world systems remain unimplemented.

## Confirmed visual direction (2026-11-01)

Visual work follows the owner's **Living Comic-Book Fantasy** direction: bold charcoal ink, calm bases, selective glow and deliberate readable motion. The owner explicitly requests that combat visuals align with the lobby, rather than become a separate UI style. See [source research and current combat translation](research/charge-art-direction.md); individual effect approval remains a playtest decision.

The owner set the art direction:**Living Comic-Book Fantasy**, with bold dark ink outlines and stylised painted textures (Borderlands-inspired) and an underground fantasy atmosphere with calm base colours and selective vibrant accents (Slugterra-inspired). Everything is original. Exact palette, outline method and treatment are still to be chosen. See the [style guide](art/style-guide.md). A lobby is planned as an underground outpost in an ancient cavern ([roadmap](art/lobby-roadmap.md)); its layout is Considering.

## Considering

Fast, skill-based PvP; collectible creatures; transformations; advanced movement; abilities; an underground world. Skiing, sliding, bunny hopping, cinematics, evolution and exploration are examples to investigate, not requirements.

## Unresolved

Movement model, camera, final combat rules, creature abilities, progression, game modes, multiplayer scale, final palette/treatment and world structure. See [open questions](questions.md).

## Implemented/verified

The foundation, isolated movement/temporary combat target range, creature technical pipeline and lobby look tests are implemented with offline checks and limited Studio evidence. The target range remains a test area, not the shipped experience. See [foundation evidence](verification.md), [movement results](movement-verification.md) and [combat/current owner choices](combat.md#current-owner-decision-and-acceptance) for actual checks and limits; subjective movement quality and broader multiplayer/device behavior still require playtesting.

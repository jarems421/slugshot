# Vision

## Confirmed

Slugshot is an original Roblox multiplayer creature-combat project. The verified development foundation is preserved. The owner authorizes isolated movement research, a playable sandbox, relevant free resource evaluation and the original Rocklock cavern greybox (2026-10-30). The later spaciousness feedback governs the map iteration; final environment/combat design is still open. Use original or appropriately licensed IP. Prototype mechanics, controls and camera parameters are not permanent design decisions; final combat/creature/world systems remain unimplemented.

## Considering

Fast, skill-based PvP; collectible creatures; transformations; advanced movement; abilities; an underground world. Skiing, sliding, bunny hopping, cinematics, evolution and exploration are examples to investigate, not requirements.

## Unresolved

Movement model, camera, combat rules, creature identity, abilities, progression, game modes, multiplayer scale, art style and world structure. See [open questions](questions.md).

## Implemented/verified

The foundation and the isolated movement sandbox. See [foundation evidence](verification.md) and [movement results](movement-verification.md) for actual checks and limits; subjective movement quality requires human playtesting.

## Source precedence

The original foundation-only request governs the preserved baseline. The later movement research/prototype request explicitly authorizes the isolated experiment, with a subsequent instruction to push the branch. The earlier PDF remains background; its mechanics are not requirements. Its old Agent Plugin guidance is superseded by built-in Studio MCP. Research/library examples never establish a final game-design decision by themselves.

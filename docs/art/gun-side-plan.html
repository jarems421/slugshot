Walking it from spawn

  1. Cross the existing gun-side rope bridge from the spawn rock, as now.
  2. Land on the mushroom knoll. It fills the whole gun side, edge to edge and out to the cavern wall, with a forest of the approved giant mushroom v2, the five grove plants, crystals and a few Floppies.
  3. The knoll wraps all the way round the hole, an uneven pit about 130 across and 120 deep, full of giant mushroom caps at many levels with vines, as in the second picture.
  4. A big bridge crosses from the knoll to the middle: about 50 long and 16 wide, heavier than the rope bridges, on root and stone supports.
  5. It ends at the shop: the pod cluster from your pictures, on a rock spire and trunk rising out of the hole, under one huge blue cap.

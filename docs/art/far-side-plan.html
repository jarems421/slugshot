Far side cave and the cavern wall

The far side becomes a cliff at the edge of the ring, looking out into a misty cave that nobody can enter. Then the cavern wall goes all the way round, where it still shows the block-out panels.

Where it goes

This is the real map from layout.json, to scale. The far side runs from 135° to 225°, straight across from the portals. Its rope bridge lands in the middle, so from spawn the bridge already points at the cave.

  • Cliff edge, with a low rock lip and an invisible barrier
  • The cave beyond the wall. Look only, out to about 560 studs from the centre
  • Wall to build, about 220° of the circle and 225 studs high
  • Wall already hidden by the grove mountain (52° to 128°)

The mountain and volcano outlines are approximate. The map shows option B's mouth, which is the one I recommend.

Side view along the far bridge

Drawn to scale, from the Maelstrom out to the cave. The orange mark is a player. You walk about 185 studs from the bridge to the edge. The ground drops away under the lip, and mist hides the bottom. The wall: three shapes

The wall is the one thing visible from everywhere, so it should sit back and frame the zones, not compete with them. All three start from the approved rock wall v6: its rounded masses, ink fractures, carved glowing cracks and moss, scaled up to the cavern. Each zone gets its own dressing at the foot of the wall (ember tones by the portals, moss and mushrooms by the grove, plum roots and bead vines on the gun side), and the top joins the ceiling's hanging rock curtain.

1 · Stacked masses

Recommended

The approved wall's big rounded masses, stacked to the ceiling, with crystal clusters and cupped-shelf mushrooms in the hollows. This is the closest to work you've already approved.

2 · Buttresses

Tall rounded rock columns rising into the ceiling, with deep recesses between them glowing with crystals. Gives the room a rhythm and makes it feel taller.

3 · Overgrown

The rock mostly hidden by giant roots, sideways mushrooms and vines climbing to the ceiling, like the gun side's hole wall but all the way round. The richest, and the heaviest for phones.

Rules I'm carrying over

  • Can't go inAn invisible barrier 60 studs high along the edge, as round the gun side's hole, so nobody lands in the cave and sees it up close. The camera and wall-runs don't catch on it.
  • Reuse what's approvedRock wall v6, the Cyan cluster and Violet spire crystals, giant mushroom v2, cupped shelves, bead vines, and the hole's five drifting mist layers. Only the cliff, arch and spires are new.
  • Mist stays localThe style guide keeps fog and exposure the same in every zone, so the mist lives inside the cave and the drop. It never fogs the hub.
  • No horizontal strataYou turned down carved bands across the rock wall before, so the tall wall avoids rows and rings of lines.
  • Far side groundIt's still block-out. My proposal is pale, weathered stone with damp moss in the hollows and a few small crystals near the edge, kept sparse so the view is the star.
  • PhonesThe lobby is already over budget, at about 1.3M triangles against 200k. I'll aim for under 40k for the cave and 120k for the whole wall, by repeating a few wall pieces round the circle.

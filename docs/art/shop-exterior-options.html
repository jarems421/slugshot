Gun side · the shop · outside · round 4

The Burrow Exchange, outside

Your pick, clustered pods, with your notes in: the rock path now lines up with the doorway, a small uneven cobble mat leads from the last stone to the door, the cap is whole and a little higher, and the trunk climbs further and fans out wide beneath it. The pods are now smooth, clean shells: no metal bands, no painted lines, finer outlines, a single clean trim round the door, and the sign plate above it. Every porthole is now a real window: an opening in the shell with a metal rim, glowing glass set back inside and a cross of bars, two of them flanking the door. The stray marks by the door and the black smudges where the pods meet are gone; only clean outlines remain. On the spire, the roots now come out of the rock under the platform and dive in and out on the way down.

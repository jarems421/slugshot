Design idea

Painted, ink-edged rock; molten amber flowing through fractured ridges; red, gold and orange library crystal designs scattered among the cave mushrooms.

Scale guide

Scale proposal: roughly 135 studs tall and about 175 studs across. The small pale figure near the base indicates player scale.

Review status

Volcano design approved. Layout fitting, the walking route and the final game build follow separately.

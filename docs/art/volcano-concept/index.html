The Ember Crown

A first volcano concept for the lobby. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, or use the view buttons.

Views

Design idea

Dark, layered cavern rock surrounds a broken, glowing crater. A wide stone path climbs the outside toward the rim, with a few warm fissures and embedded crystal seams.

Scale guide

Based on the lobby blockout: roughly 135 studs tall and 130 studs across. The small pale figure near the base indicates player scale.

Review status

Concept only. Colours, path, shape and name are all open to change. Lava effects, collision, lighting and performance must be checked separately in Roblox.

# Slugterra environment research

Research into Slugterra's world (terrain, flora, structures, technology, mood) to inform Slugshot's lobby and map. Requested by the owner on 2026-10-31. This note studies **categories and atmosphere** only. Every Slugshot asset is an original design ([style guide](../art/style-guide.md#8-asset-quality-bar-not-ai-slop)). Franchise names, characters, places and designs belong to their rights holders and must not appear in the game.

## Method and limits

- Web searches on 2026-10-31. The SlugTerra Fandom wiki (HTTP 402) and TV Tropes (HTTP 403) refused direct fetches, so wiki facts come from search-result excerpts only.
- **Search-confirmed** items cite a source. **From memory** items are recollections of the show that were not re-verified this session. Treat them as leads to check against screenshots or episodes before relying on specifics.
- No official environment art book or series bible was found.

## The world: search-confirmed

- A vast underground world beneath the surface and above the darker, dangerous **Deep Caverns** ([wiki location page](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/SlugTerra_(location)), [Deep Caverns](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/The_Deep_Caverns)).
- Traditionally **99 Caverns**, each with its own unique slug species and history; the later **Eastern Caverns** lie beyond ([99 Caverns](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/The_99_Caverns), [Eastern Caverns](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/The_Eastern_Caverns)). The wiki also groups caverns into regions (e.g. Western Caverns, Shadow Mines) and keeps a category for **landmarks**, such as the rock formation Stalagmite 17 ([landmarks](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Category:99_Caverns'_Landmarks), [regions](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Category:99_Caverns'_Regions)).
- **Each cavern has its own biome identity.** Examples: Cavern Seas (a large deep underground sea), Chillbore (frozen, northern), Crystal Cavern (home of a crystal slug species), Quiet Lawn, Herringbone, Shadow Mines ([Cavern Seas](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Cavern_Seas), [Quiet Lawn](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Quiet_Lawn_Cavern), [Geoshard/Crystal Cavern](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Geoshard)).
- Genre: action, science fantasy, comedy **and Western** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra)). A weaker source says the creator wanted a Western set in a subterranean fantasy world.
- Produced by Nerd Corps Entertainment, Vancouver, in CG animation ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra)).

## Flora: search-confirmed

From the wiki's plant-life excerpts ([location page](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/SlugTerra_(location))):

- **Giant mushrooms:** "larger than normal and can be found all over". This is the signature flora; TV Tropes also lists "giant underground mushrooms" as a trope of the show.
- **Grasping seaweed** on the floors of underwater caverns, which wraps around things.
- **Flowering vines** with blossoms.
- **Named mushroom varieties**, including a gentle one that later became defensive and spiky.
- **Blossom trees** with pink flowers in an Eastern cavern.

Takeaway: flora is **oversized, characterful and sometimes behaves like creatures**. Each biome gets its own plants.

## Design lessons for Slugshot

1. **Every area is a distinct biome with its own creatures, flora and colour.** This matches the owner's plan for a map of merging biomes. The lobby can preview them.
2. **Frontier Western meets underground fantasy.** Slingers as gunslingers suit our blaster duels. Considering: borrow the *genre* feel (showdowns, frontier outposts, bounty boards, saloon-like gathering spots), not the show's designs.
3. **Giant mushrooms are the signature flora.** Oversized, characterful and varied by biome. Our designs need their own silhouettes.
4. **Natural vs built contrast.** Soft glowing caverns, frontier hand-built outposts and cold industrial strongholds each tell a story through shape and light.
5. **Technology glows with creature energy.** Slug-powered lights, conduits and canisters let the industrial kit share the world's glow.
6. **Vertical, layered spaces.** Shafts, ledges, bridges and waterfalls give caves scale and wonder.
7. **Slugs are everywhere and visible:** in canisters, habitats, on rocks, in the wild. Our lobby Floppies and the slug exhibition follow this.

## What to avoid copying

Mecha beasts as a concept, the hero's hideout layout, named caverns, the villain fortress, Slug Fu, any slug species, the show's blaster designs and logos. Slugshot's equivalents need their own names, shapes and fiction.

# Rocklock / Slugterra visual comparison

2026-10-30. **Confirmed request:** compare Rocklock with actual Slugterra imagery and identify how to improve the cavern's look. The earlier request for open combat space, crystal light sources and collision that follows the visible rocks still applies. The owner has not approved a new art pass from these recommendations.

## Material reviewed

- [RTVE Slugterra series image](https://img.rtve.es/p/74752/imgportada/?w=1600): inspected the actual image. Blue and violet cavern, broad irregular dark rocks, textured purple ground and bright cyan crystal-like growth at the edge of the shot. This is a promotional image, not proof that every episode uses this palette.
- [Prime Video season 3 image](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/S/pv-target-images/47c38716aa4b94253379fd427d589458c520d09bd5723700cd3a51e4413bcc3c.jpg): inspected the actual image. A cluster of sharply pointed, overlapping, icy blue, internally varied facets forms the focus. It is a close-up, not evidence for a whole cavern's layout.
- [AlloCiné series image](https://www.allocine.fr/series/ficheserie-12211/photos/detail/?cmediafile=21720068): inspected the linked promotional image. Dark curved organic silhouettes in the foreground layer against a brighter blue background; the environment is an underground forest, not a bare collapsed cavern.
- [Plex episode still](https://watch.plex.tv/show/slugterra/season/1/episode/26): inspected the linked image. This is a darker, red-accented shot and is included as evidence that the show varies its lighting; it is not a universal palette target.
- The [official Slugterra watch page](https://www.slugterra.com/pages/episodes) and [No Exit episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vWlro6it6uY) are additional viewing references. Their video contents were not watched or analyzed frame by frame for this comparison.
- Rocklock: inspected the earlier [establishing](../evidence/rocklock/02-establishing.png), [centre](../evidence/rocklock/03-player-centre.png), [west route](../evidence/rocklock/04-west-route.png) Blender renders and [crystal lineup](../evidence/rocklock/crystal-variants.png); also reviewed current map geometry and placement source. The rock-only renders predate the crystal lighting and do not show the current Studio build. Studio capture of the current build was blank, so exact current in-game color, crystal visibility and lighting are **unresolved**.

Reference images are used for visual study only and are not shipped in the project. Model original geometry and materials; do not copy franchise characters, logos, locations or assets.

## Comparison and proposed changes

| Area | Observed comparison | Considering for Rocklock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main chamber | The inspected Rocklock views read as a flat rectangular room with straight, repeated shelves and large plain wall panels. The RTVE image frames the action with broader uneven, rounded-angular rock masses. | Reshape the perimeter and roof into a few large asymmetrical rock layers, overhangs and tapered ridges. Retain the 212.8 × 186.2 footprint, broad combat lanes and character clearance; add visual complexity chiefly above and around the edges. |
| Central landmark | The current centre reads as stacked blocks and a straight bridge in the older render. The reference rocks read as naturally joined large masses. | Rework the arch into a fractured formation with irregular tapered shoulders and interlocking stones, while retaining the existing routes and leaving a generous underpass. Avoid adding small pillars or cover to the floor. |
| Surface finish | The older Rocklock render is mostly uniform grey and flat. The RTVE shot has visible roughness and color variation on the ground and rocks. | Develop a restrained stylized stone finish: broad blue-slate and violet-brown variation, larger strata, chips and cracks. Keep the fighting floor legible and reasonably even. Any texture/material assets must be original or appropriately licensed. |
| Crystals | The close-up shows a dense, irregular cluster with overlapping pointed shards and internal brightness variations. Rocklock's Blender lineup has five clean, regular prism designs; actual in-game look has not been visually captured. | Make shard silhouettes less regular and group a few into naturally embedded formations, with color and brightness variation within each cluster. Keep crystal-hosted lighting, and place the formations at the perimeter or above head height to protect movement and sightlines. |
| Lighting and depth | The RTVE image uses cool blues and purples with bright cyan accents; the other reviewed stills show different color moods. The old Rocklock renders use broad neutral preview lights, whereas the current build has crystal lighting and color grade but no usable capture. | Test a blue-violet stone palette, crystal-local light and subtle depth falloff in the current Studio place. Ensure fighters, projectiles and floor edges stay readable; do not darken the whole arena based on a promotional image. |
| Silhouette and framing | The underground-forest image uses layered irregular shapes and brighter background separation. Rocklock's straight side wall and shelf dominate the earlier player view. | Break up the skyline with uneven ceiling silhouettes and a few recessed wall contours. Keep background depth outside the playable footprint or integrated into the walls, with no new obstacles in the open lanes. |

**Priority recommendation:** first make one representative wall/shelf section and the central arch more natural, then review them from player height with the current crystals before applying the style across the map. This tests the silhouette and finish without filling the arena. The current 4,800 collidable rock facets and 6,394 total parts already warrant profiling: increasing their count may hurt performance. Any revised visible shape must have correspondingly accurate collision and must be checked for seams, wall movement and camera/limb clipping. These are proposed visual changes, not implemented or tested.

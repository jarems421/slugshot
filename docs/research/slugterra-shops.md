# Slugterra shop references, 2026-11-01

Owner requested research into actual shops before an original Slugshot general-shop concept. The building should be medium to moderately large and become the future shopping hub, rather than an exclusively gun-focused shop. This research guides categories and visual language; no franchise mesh, texture, logo, character or building layout is copied into the concept.

## Confirmed sources

- **Barton and Spinks** is a retailer in *The Trade*. The [location article](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Barton_and_Spinks) describes furniture, blaster upgrades and parts among its goods; [Spinks](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Spinks) identifies the proprietor. The [official WildBrain episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mY9KVNmy11w) independently establishes the gear-trading premise. This is a suitable general-retailer reference, not evidence that Slugshot should adopt that shop's trading rules.
- **Official episode visual reference:** public storyboard thumbnails from the official upload, sampled at 10-second intervals, were inspected locally. Around [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mY9KVNmy11w&t=350s) a commercial street is visible; around [6:00 onward](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mY9KVNmy11w&t=360s) shop walls, mounted equipment, shelving, a sales counter and a terminal are visible. These are thumbnail observations, not a complete high-resolution architectural survey of the episode.
- **Production layout reference:** [Lazarino Baarde's portfolio](https://www.coroflot.com/storyboarder/CONCEPT-LAY-OUTS-n-SCENE-PLANS) labels several drawings as *Slugterra: Eastern Caverns*. The first labelled layout was inspected: an organic cavern setting with exaggerated trunk forms, paths and a small arched opening. This is broader environment reference, not an identified shop design.
- [CreatureBox](https://www.creaturebox.com/slugterra/) describes its participation in guiding style and designing characters, slugs and vehicles. It supports production provenance; its gallery was not treated as evidence of a particular storefront.

## Observations and translation into the original concept

| Observed visual cue | Original Slugshot interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Muted green/purple commercial surfaces and rounded/ribbed facade shapes in the official street thumbnails | Slate shell, oxidised teal arched roof and chunky copper ribs |
| Heavy arched outpost openings elsewhere in the episode, and practical shop counters | A broad inward-opening entrance, back service counter and clear central aisle |
| Equipment on walls and varied retail goods | Display bays, shelf packs, supply cases, maps and energy canisters, with room for future categories |
| Technical display/terminal in the shop | A small proposed counter terminal; no currency, inventory or purchase backend |
| Existing Slugshot treatment: ink and painted variation with selective glow | Dark outlines, painted metal/wood/stone, warm lantern cores, restrained mint energy accents and the reusable cave fern |

**Considering:** *The Burrow Exchange* is the new working name for this concept. It replaces no confirmed name; the earlier *Burrow & Barrel* was only a suggestion. The concept is an original rounded trading post, not a reconstruction of Barton and Spinks.

## Limits and provenance

The Fandom article text was readable through web search/open, but its hosted storefront image returned a JavaScript/cookie challenge. That image was not inspected and was not bypassed. Official episode thumbnails and the production layout image were inspected instead. Downloaded reference material remains under ignored `build/research/shop`; it is not shipped with the preview or used as a model texture.

**Unresolved:** owner judgement of style fit, name, proportions, interior layout and final scale. Research does not establish final game-shop behaviour or NPC design.

## Owner-supplied house references (2026-11-02)

**Confirmed visual observations:** the two supplied screenshots show rounded pale mineral/organic pods around a tree, small cyan portholes, a broad blue fungal canopy and saturated exotic plants. This is the owner's intended architecture category, superseding the first shop's conventional barrel roof. No claim is made that these images depict Barton and Spinks. The new shop uses an original arrangement; supplied franchise imagery is not hosted or packaged.

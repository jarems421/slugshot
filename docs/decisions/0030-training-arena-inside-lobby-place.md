# 0030: Training arena lives inside the lobby place

**Status:** Implemented for owner review, 2026-11-03.

## Context

The owner wants to walk through the training portal with a smooth transition and no freeze. `TeleportService` between places needs a published experience, does not work in Studio and shows a loading screen.

## Decision

Build the arena in the lobby's place, 6000 studs from the lobby, generated by `tools/training/build_game.py` from the lobby and combat range projects. The portal is a client wash over a server-validated move. Only the generated copy of the movement server gains a `TrainingWarp` exception to its motion check. Guns and the combat HUD are limited to the arena by `InTraining`. Movement, combat and lobby source are unchanged.

## Consequences

The arena's art is always loaded, so lobby and arena costs add up on phones. The move is seamless and testable in Studio. If mobile cost is too high, the arena can move to its own place at the price of a loading screen. Supersedes the review place in ADR 0023.

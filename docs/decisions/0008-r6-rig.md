# ADR 0008: The R6 rig, with clips converted from R15

Date: 2026-10-29
Status: Accepted for the experiment only; reconsider after the owner's v5.0 playtest
Approval evidence: owner, 2026-10-29: "switch to the real R6 rig … continue optimizing it comparing it to the clips … make sure it's identical … and it matches the rest of the movements already in place"
Builds on: [ADR 0007](0007-block-limbs-and-original-run.md)

## Context

The owner's reference (Huss Valley) uses the classic R6 rig; the avatar line-up at 5:40 of the reference video shows it. R6 limbs are single blocks with no hands, elbows or knees. The arms pivot a quarter of the way down their inner face, so their tops swing out past the shoulder. The owner described this as limbs looking "disconnected" and "free". R15 with locked joints (ADR 0007) cannot reproduce it: R15 limbs pivot at their tops.

## Decision

- **Rig.** Characters spawn as R6 (`Config.Avatar.RigType`). The R15 path still works if the setting is changed back.
- **Clips stay in R15 terms.** No clip data changes. `Presentation.toR6` converts each final pose:
  - The single torso takes Root then Waist, turning about the hip and waist points as on R15 (the R6 root joint pivots at the torso centre).
  - The legs are re-expressed against that torso.
  - The arms and neck carry over.
  - Elbows, knees, wrists and ankles simply do not exist.
- **Joint frames.** R6 Motor6Ds carry a 90° turn in C0 and C1, so a pose is applied as `C0r⁻¹ · pose · C1r`. Forward kinematics (ground contact) uses only the pivot positions. R15 joints, which have no turn, are unaffected.
- **Heights.** R6 ignores `HipHeight`. `Clips.standHeight` (leg length plus half the root) and `Clips.hipUnit` (leg length, 2 studs) replace the R15 formulas in presentation, traversal, the client and server ground checks.
- **Run.** Run and Sprint are one reference cycle (`snapRun`, 60 fps, 0.47 s per two steps), matched frame by frame to the reference with a solid-block R6 renderer. Each limb has its own leave/pass/arrive timing, and limbs swing outward as they go back.

## Consequences

- R15-only adjustments are now inert: hidden hands and `ArmScale`.
- **Limb folding (v5.1).** Each two-segment limb in a clip becomes one block aimed from its root to its end (`Config.Animation.FoldLimbs`, `Presentation.chord`), keeping the upper segment's twist. This replaces `RigidJoints`, which kept the thigh's angle: the idle's slightly bent knees tipped the straight R6 legs forward, and floor contact then lifted the body onto them, so it looked like floating. Folding also keeps feet under the body in landings and jumps.
- Mocap one-shots (jumps, landings, flips, rolls, mantles, vault, wall kicks) play through the conversion. In renders, idle, rolls and flips read acceptably on R6. The mocap running and standing jumps and the landing do not: their knee squats become waist folds and their tucks become stiff pikes. They need R6 block versions to match the run.
- The wall-run hand reach uses R6 arm geometry (pivot to arm end, 1.5 studs).

## Addendum (2026-10-30, v7.0)

The pose-to-pose snapRun read as stiff and choppy in play and has been replaced by a continuous R6 run generator (lowRun) with three candidate styles chosen in the lab. The R6 rig and the R15-to-R6 conversion are unchanged.

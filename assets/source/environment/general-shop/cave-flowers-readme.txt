Slugshot original cave flowers - concept assets, study 02, 2026-11-02
Tidefan Bloom (fan): broad fluted blue fan with curled outer tips and mint veins.
Mistbell (bell): branching stems carry three hanging violet bells and mint centres.
Coil Orchid (orchid): six curled plum petals around a luminous mint stamen crown.
Each GLB contains one plant at its own ground-root origin, vertex-colour paint and charcoal ink geometry. Authored units are studs; retain relative dimensions when importing. All GLB mesh coordinates are baked; no external textures are needed.
Source: cave-flowers.js. create(THREE,{variant:'fan'|'bell'|'orchid'}). Three.js r159 is vendored under THREE-LICENSE.txt. GLB export calls the shared SlugshotFern.toGLB function from fern-asset.js. No franchise artwork/meshes are included.
These are editable browser concept meshes, not Blender production sources or Studio-verified assets. Glow and smoothing may differ between viewers; recreate local light/glow in Roblox. Review open petal surfaces, collisions, mobile budgets and final scale in production. No uploads or Studio changes have been made.

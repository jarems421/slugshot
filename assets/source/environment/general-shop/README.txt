The Burrow Exchange - original Slugshot general shop concept

The working name and first concept await owner approval.
Authored browser source: shop-preview.js. It is also tracked in the Sites source repository.
It requires Three.js r159 and the current gallery's preview UI; call SlugshotShopPreview.start({THREE,renderer,stage,Q}).
The shell, inward-opening door pivots, removable roof, display bays, furnishings and render materials are authored here. No franchise assets or third-party models are included.
The counter reserves a future shopkeeper position; no game purchasing system or NPC behaviour is implemented.
Dimensions proposed: around 50 x 43 studs, 27 tall. The outer presentation slab is slightly larger.
Research: docs/research/slugterra-shops.md. Design and evidence: docs/art/general-shop.md.
Blender/Roblox production and engine validation are still pending.

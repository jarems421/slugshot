# Burpy (Slugterra): identity, canon facts and visual design

**Evidence limits, applying to every section.** Direct fetches of slugterra.fandom.com returned HTTP 402 and the BreezeWiki mirror returned an access-denied page, so no wiki page was read in full. Wiki claims below come from search-engine summaries of those pages. Those summaries blend several results, so a citation to a wiki URL means "the search surfaced this from that page", not "I read it there". Only the Wikipedia pages and one ToyWiz listing were fetched directly. No episode was watched and no image was viewed. Fan-wiki text is secondary evidence for show canon. Labels used: **[Wikipedia]**, **[Fan wiki]**, **[Retail]**.

## Species name, classification and spelling

### Takeaway
Burpy is an individual slug whose species is Infurnus, a rare fire-type. "Burpy" is the spelling in every source found. No source for a catalogue number was found.

### Cited Findings
- [Fan wiki] Burpy is an Infurnus from the 99 Caverns and the signature slug of protagonist Eli Shane — [SlugTerra Wiki: Burpy](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Burpy), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] Infurnus is a rare fire-type, limited to one per set of caverns — [SlugTerra Wiki: Infurnus](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Infurnus), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] Species abilities are given as devastating fireballs and heat shields — [SlugTerra Wiki: Infurnus](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Infurnus), via search index.
- [Retail] Burpy is the name used on licensed Jakks Pacific products, including a Series 1 mini-figure 2-pack (March 2013) — [ToyWiz listing](https://toywiz.com/slugterra-series-1-burpy-joules-mini-figure-2-pack/) (seen as a search summary), and a plush (January 2014) — [ToyWiz listing](https://toywiz.com/slugterra-burpy-plush/) (fetched).
- [Wikipedia] The species is spelled "Infernus" in one plot summary, against "Infurnus" on the fan wiki — [Wikipedia: Ghoul from Beyond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra:_Ghoul_from_Beyond). Another Wikipedia article uses "Infurnuses" — [Wikipedia: Return of the Elementals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra:_Return_of_the_Elementals).
- [Wikipedia] Series context: it ran from 3 September 2012 to 25 October 2016, with Nerd Corps producing seasons 1-4 and DHX Media producing seasons 5-6 — [Wikipedia: Slugterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra).

### Inferences
- "Burpee" looks like a simple misspelling. It appeared in none of the results, though I did not search for it on its own.
- "Infernus" on Wikipedia is probably an editing variant, and "Infurnus" is the better-supported form.

### Gaps
- Catalogue number: none found. No source reviewed mentions one, so it may not exist.
- The Infurnus wiki page's own class label could not be read directly.

## Protoform appearance

### Takeaway
The sources agree on a dark-orange slug with yellow and tan markings, yellow-tipped antennae, a tan flame-patterned belly and a flame-like mark on the head. The head mark's shape is disputed. Eyes, mouth and exact size are undocumented in what I could reach.

### Cited Findings
- [Fan wiki] Infurnus is dark orange with yellow and tan markings — [SlugTerra Wiki: Infurnus](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Infurnus), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] The belly is small, tan and flame-patterned — same source, via search index.
- [Fan wiki] Two antennae sit on top of the head with yellow tips — same source, via search index.
- [Fan wiki] A small two-tipped yellow flame sits on top of the head — same source, via search index.
- [Fan wiki] Burpy individually is described as orange-skinned with flame designs on the head and stomach — [SlugTerra Wiki: Burpy](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Burpy), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] The Infurnus protoform is said to look like a Flaringo apart from the colour scheme — [SlugTerra Wiki: Infurnus](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Infurnus), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] **Conflict on the head mark:** one page describes Burpy's head mark as a lightning bolt, in contrast with the small flame on Joo-Joo's head — [SlugTerra Wiki: Joo-Joo](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Joo-Joo), via search index. The Infurnus page describes a two-tipped yellow flame as the species mark (above).
- [Fan wiki] Another individual difference: Joo-Joo's usual belly mark is described as a fireworks-style flame, unlike Burpy's — [SlugTerra Wiki: Joo-Joo](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Joo-Joo), via search index.
- [Retail] A used-lot listing gives the mini figures as 1-2 cm, which is toy scale, not in-show scale — [eBay.de listing](https://www.ebay.de/itm/336236484539), via search index.

### Inferences
- The "lightning bolt" and "two-tipped flame" may be one jagged mark described two ways. Checking a screenshot would settle it.
- The Flaringo comparison suggests the Infurnus body plan is not unique, so the colour and marking scheme may carry more of its identity than its basic shape.

### Gaps
- Eye shape and colour, mouth, body proportions and in-show size relative to other slugs: no citable description was found. The designer should compare these against official artwork or episode frames.
- No hex or official colour values were found. All colours above are verbal descriptions.

## Velocimorph and other forms

### Takeaway
At velocity the slug becomes a flaming phoenix-dragon hybrid with burning wings and tail. Burpy later gains a permanent "megamorph" change. The ghoul form of the species is called Darkfurnus, but whether Burpy himself is ever ghouled is contested.

### Cited Findings
- [Wikipedia] Slugs transform into powerful beasts on reaching 100 MPH — [Wikipedia: Slugterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra).
- [Fan wiki] The Infurnus velocimorph takes on phoenix-dragon traits, with its tail and wings on fire — [SlugTerra Wiki: Infurnus](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Infurnus), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] Velocimorphs generally resemble their protoform, the Lavalynx being a noted exception — [SlugTerra Wiki: Slugs](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugs), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] A mega velocimorph comes at 200 MPH and slightly alters features and colour markings — [SlugTerra Wiki: Slugs](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugs), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] Burpy's first megamorph happened when Eli fired him while using mecha upgrades travelling at 100 MPH, so the speeds stacked. It boosted his power, changed his look slightly, and he stayed in that form — [SlugTerra Wiki: Megamorph](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Megamorph), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] **Conflict on who was first:** some wiki text claims Joo-Joo megamorphed over 2,000 years ago, against Burpy being the "first known" — [SlugTerra Wiki: Joo-Joo](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Joo-Joo), via search index. "First known" could allow for both.
- [Fan wiki] Darkfurnus is the name of the ghouled Infurnus — [SlugTerra Wiki: Darkfurnus](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Darkfurnus), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] In *Ghoul from Beyond*, the possessed Joo-Joo appears as a Darkfurnus, reportedly sharing Burpy's belly flame instead of the usual fireworks mark — [SlugTerra Wiki: Joo-Joo](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Joo-Joo), via search index.
- [Fan wiki vs Wikipedia] **Conflict on Ghoul Burpy:** a wiki summary says a ghoul slug (a Goon Doc) ghoulified Burpy in *Ghoul from Beyond* and he attacked the Shane Gang — [SlugTerra Wiki: Burpy](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Burpy), via search index. The Wikipedia plot does not mention this. It has the Goon controlling Eli, with Burpy returning with Doc to break that control — [Wikipedia: Ghoul from Beyond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra:_Ghoul_from_Beyond). The two could both be true, since a plot summary may omit events.
- [Wikipedia] In *Return of the Elementals* (US premiere 2 August 2014), the Fire Elemental being ghouled also turns the Infurnuses into ghouls — [Wikipedia: Return of the Elementals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra:_Return_of_the_Elementals). The article does not name Burpy.
- [Retail] Blaster Evo dart packs are listed in "V1" and "V2" Burpy versions, so merchandise has more than one version — [V1 pack](https://toywiz.com/slugterra-blaster-evo-dart-transforming-slug-darts-3-pack-exclusive-burpy-v1-joules-spinner/) and [V2 pack](https://toywiz.com/slugterra-blaster-evo-dart-transforming-slug-darts-3-pack-exclusive-burpy-v2-doc-v2-frightgeist-v2/), via search index. What differs between them is not stated.

### Inferences
- The 100 and 200 MPH figures are not a real conflict, though one search summary framed them that way. They appear to be separate thresholds for velocimorph and mega velocimorph.
- The silhouette signature is winged, long-tailed and on fire, so a flaming bird-dragon is the shape an original design most needs to avoid.

### Gaps
- Colours, flame hue and exact silhouette of the velocimorph, megamorph and Darkfurnus: no reachable source gives specifics. The Darkfurnus search returned no colour description.
- *Slugterra: Into the Shadows* and Slugisodes: no Burpy-specific form was found. An [Into the Shadows wiki page](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugterra:_Into_the_Shadows) surfaced but could not be read.

## Personality, relationship, plot role and status

### Takeaway
Burpy is Will Shane's slug passed to his son Eli, and he serves as Eli's most loyal companion and number-one slug. His hero status comes from this story role and from the Infurnus's link to cavern champions. I found no canon statement ranking him the most powerful.

### Cited Findings
- [Fan wiki] Burpy belonged to Will Shane's arsenal and was sent to find Eli just before Will went missing — [SlugTerra Wiki: Burpy](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Burpy), via search index.
- [Episode listing, source unclear] In the premiere, Will tells his slug to find Eli and deliver a letter. The same text says "In U.S., it's Burpy", hinting at a regional name difference — surfaced in a search on these terms; the summary did not attribute it, and the nearest candidates are [TheTVDB](https://thetvdb.com/series/slugterra/episodes/4391326) and [SensCritique](https://www.senscritique.com/serie/slugterra_les_mondes_souterrains/15916011/S2/E3). Treat as weak.
- [Wikipedia] Eli becomes the new Shane of Slugterra after his father, "The Protector of Slugterra", goes missing, sent away by Dr. Blakk — [Wikipedia: Slugterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra).
- [Fan wiki] Burpy is described as extremely loyal, cleverer than the average slug, and using moves most people have never seen — [SlugTerra Wiki: Burpy](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Burpy), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] He is called Eli's number-one slug and best friend — [SlugTerra Wiki: Burpy](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Burpy), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] Infurnus slugs go to the champion and protector of each set of caverns. Junjie's Joo-Joo is the Eastern Caverns counterpart — [SlugTerra Wiki: Infurnus](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Infurnus) and [Joo-Joo](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Joo-Joo), via search index.
- [Wikipedia] In *Ghoul from Beyond*, Burpy transforms to drive off the Marauders, goes after Doc in the Slug ways, and returns to free Eli from the Goon — [Wikipedia: Ghoul from Beyond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra:_Ghoul_from_Beyond).
- [Wikipedia] All slugs are said to descend from five Elementals: Earth, Fire, Water, Wind and Energy — [Wikipedia: Return of the Elementals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra:_Return_of_the_Elementals). That article names no Burpy role.
- [Low quality] A search result titled "Is Burpy an Elemental Slug?" exists, but it returned HTTP 403 and looks like a generic content-farm article, so it is not relied on — [enviroliteracy.org](https://enviroliteracy.org/animals/is-burpy-a-elemental-slug/).

### Inferences
- Burpy is probably not one of the five Elementals: the Wikipedia plot treats the Fire Elemental and the Infurnuses as separate, the latter affected through descent. No source reviewed states this outright.
- "Hero slug" reads as a narrative role made of father-to-son inheritance, loyalty, cleverness and first-megamorph status, reinforced by toy-line presence. Burpy recurs across the Jakks Pacific lines listed on ToyWiz.
- "Rare" has a concrete fan-wiki basis in the one-per-caverns rule. "Most powerful" does not, and should be treated as fan sentiment at most.

### Gaps
- No canon power ranking was found. A [fan poll page](https://www.thetoptens.com/television/slugterra-slugs/) surfaced but was not read, and it would only show popularity anyway.
- The official Slugterra site or archive was not reached, so no first-party character description is available.
- Show-level personality details such as sounds and mannerisms are undocumented here.

## Recognisable traits and how to stay distinct

### Takeaway
The likely identifiers are the orange-yellow-tan scheme, the yellow head flame or bolt, the tan flame belly, the yellow antenna tips, and the winged burning phoenix-dragon form. An original fire slug should change several of these together, not just recolour. This section is design inference, not sourced canon, and no images were viewed to confirm it.

### Cited Findings
- [Fan wiki] Wiki descriptions use head mark and belly mark to tell individual Infurnuses apart (Burpy versus Joo-Joo), showing these marks work as identity signals in-universe — [SlugTerra Wiki: Joo-Joo](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Joo-Joo), via search index.
- [Fan wiki] Fan-made recolours of the Infurnus exist, as a fan creation and not canon — [Multiversal Madness on Tumblr](https://multiversal-madness.tumblr.com/post/674421586173050880/here-are-some-different-coloured-infurnus-slugs), via search index.

### Inferences
Suggested changes to keep an original design distinct, all proposals for the designer:

| Burpy trait (per sources above) | What to avoid | Possible direction |
|---|---|---|
| Dark orange with yellow and tan | The same three-colour scheme | A different fire logic, such as ember-black with coal cracks or a magenta or teal flame, within the project's style guide |
| Yellow flame or bolt on top of the head | Any single emblem on the crown | Heat expressed through form, such as a vent or a smouldering ridge |
| Tan flame-patterned belly | A flame icon on the stomach | A plain belly, or a different pattern logic and placement |
| Two yellow-tipped antennae | Yellow tips on otherwise body-coloured stalks | A distinct antenna shape or tip treatment |
| Flaming phoenix-dragon with burning wings and tail | Winged fire bird-dragon | A non-avian silhouette, such as a fire-spitting serpent, a comet, or a magma creature |
| Fireball and heat-shield kit | Copying the pairing as signature moves | A different mechanic, such as burn trails or a delayed ignition |
| Father's slug inherited by the hero, one per region | The same backstory | Original lore |
| Name built on a bodily-noise joke | A burp-style name | A name with its own concept |

- Changing only hue would leave the layout of head mark, belly mark and antenna tips intact, so the resemblance would probably survive. Change layout and transformed silhouette as well.
- Fire element and a slug body alone are broad genre ideas. The closest resemblance would come from combining the specific elements in the table. This is a design judgement and not legal advice.

### Gaps
- Actual visual recognisability was not tested, because no images were viewed. Someone should compare the final concept side by side with official Burpy artwork as a private reference check, without copying or reusing the art.

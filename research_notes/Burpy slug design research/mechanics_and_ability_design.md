# Slug firing canon and per-projectile ability design

Source quality note: direct access to the SlugTerra Fandom wiki (HTTP 402), a BreezeWiki mirror (bot block) and the Splatoon wiki (HTTP 403) failed. Findings cited to those wikis come from search-indexed excerpts, not full pages, and the search tool summarised them, so the exact page for a given excerpt may be approximate. All are fan-written. Nothing here was checked against episodes.

## Canon: how slugs fire, transform, hit and return

### Takeaway
The canon threshold is **100 mph, not 100 feet per second**; no source found supports the feet-per-second figure. The transform-at-speed premise is well supported, but impact, reversion and whether one slug has several shots could not be confirmed from accessible sources.

### Cited Findings
- Slugs are tiny creatures with supernatural powers, trained as living ammunition for launchers called Blasters, and they transform when they reach velocity at 100 mph — [Wikipedia: Slugterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra)
- The episode list describes the same mechanic: slugs reach one hundred miles per hour with the aid of slugslingers' blasters — [Wikipedia: List of Slugterra episodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)
- A search aimed specifically at "feet per second" returned only mph figures — [SlugTerra Wiki: Slugs](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugs); [Shapes.inc: Slug Types](https://shapes.inc/fandom/slugterra/slug-types)
- Protoform is the base form; the velocimorph is the larger, stronger form and usually resembles the protoform, with Lavalynx as the noted exception — [SlugTerra Wiki: Slugs](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugs)
- **Flopper Slugs are the only known slugs that do not transform at velocity.** This matters because Slugshot's first creature is Flopper-inspired — [SlugTerra Wiki: Slugs](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugs)
- Second tier: at 200 mph a slug takes a mega velocimorph form. Megamorphing is described as a permanent change to a more aerodynamic protoform body, and an accelerator in the blaster makes it easier — [SlugTerra Wiki: Megamorph](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Megamorph)
- A fan wiki says blasters run on slug energy and more experienced slugs make more powerful shots (exact page not confirmed) — [SlugTerra Wiki: Slugs](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugs)
- Neo-protoform is a separate state in which a slug or ghoul is drained of energy and shrinks to a baby-like form. It is not described as a normal post-shot reversion (exact page not confirmed) — [SlugTerra Wiki search excerpts](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugs)
- Return behaviour, indirect evidence only: a stolen slug is said to be likely to return to its original slinger once fired, limiting the value of stealing slugs — [SlugTerra Wiki: Armashelt](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Armashelt)

Slugterra's own games:
- *Slug it Out 2* is a match-3 puzzle RPG, not a shooter. Puzzle moves charge the slug team and fuel signature attacks, with 100+ slugs across Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Energy and Psychic elements — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id1148766137); [APKMirror](https://apkmirror.com/apk/nightmarket-games/slugterra-slug-it-out-2)
- *Dark Waters* is an action-adventure shooter in which you play Eli and fire slugs from blasters. Reviewers rate it fun but unremarkable, and touch input handles both movement and targeting — [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/slugterra-dark-waters)
- *Dark Waters* lets you equip favourite slugs and upgrade them, and describes them as living ammo with unique abilities — [TouchArcade](https://toucharcade.com/games/slugterra-dark-waters); [Oceanol, an aggregator](https://oceanol.com/game/21972.html)
- One listing describes tap to fire and hold to charge. It may be a different Slugterra title (possibly *Slugslinger Showdown*), so treat with caution — [RAWG: Slugslinger Showdown](https://rawg.io/games/slugterra-slugslinger-showdown)

### Inferences
- 100 mph is about 146.7 ft/s, or 44.7 m/s (arithmetic). The owner's figure of 100 ft/s is about 30.5 m/s, so the two differ by about a third. If Slugshot quotes a number on screen or in docs, use mph or an invented in-world unit.
- Copying the "Flopper does not transform" fact is an IP and design consideration: Slugshot's Floppy is original and need not follow it, but the owner should choose knowingly.
- Canon's two tiers (velocimorph, then mega with an accelerator) suggest a blaster-side stat could control transformation. That is a design idea, not a canon claim.
- From the games found, none appears to offer per-shot ability selection, but the evidence is store-listing level, so this is weak.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm from accessible sources: what a slug does on impact, how it reverts after a shot, how it physically returns, how slug tubes or canisters work, and the blaster's internal acceleration mechanism.
- **The central canon question is unanswered:** whether one slug can produce different shots, and whether the slinger's aim or intent decides this. No accessible source addresses it. Answering needs the individual slug pages or episode checks.
- Unverified recollection, not sourced: slugs revert to protoform after their attack and make their own way back to the slinger. Verify before relying on it.
- "Tandem shots" appears in a wiki excerpt, but no details were retrieved.
- No controls breakdown or per-slug ability list was found for *Dark Waters*.

## Precedents: one ability per item versus several

### Takeaway
Among the precedents found, fixed bundles chosen before play are the norm. Where in-fight choosing was added (Overwatch perks), it was reported as hard to do mid-fight, which supports the owner's worry. Coverage is partial, and only some named games were sourced.

### Cited Findings
- **Splatoon:** weapons come as predefined main, sub and special sets, sold as class-like bundles rather than mix-and-match — [Nintendo Wire](https://nintendowire.com/news/2017/07/12/new-weapons-details-revealed-splatoon-2/); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/splatoon-preview)
- **Splatoon:** the same main can appear in several sets with different subs and specials, so variety comes from alternative kits — [Nintendo Wire: Famitsu interview](https://nintendowire.com/news/2017/02/13/famitsu-interview-reveals-new-splatoon-2-details/)
- **Overwatch structure:** one analysis counts at most five abilities per hero: primary, optional secondary, movement, situational utility and ultimate — [Ludus Novus](https://ludusnovus.net/2016/08/04/strange-symmetra-accretive-design-in-overwatch/)
- **Overwatch complexity:** director Aaron Keller called Lifeweaver's large kit a departure, said loadouts can get a little overwhelming, and said such abilities might previously have been split across heroes. He also values the learning curve, and said not every hero will be this complex — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/overwatch-2/hero-complexity)
- **Overwatch perks (strongest evidence for this decision):** each round you earn a minor then a major perk, press a keybind, and choose one of two options with left or right mouse. The reviewer found this hard mid-fight and advises choosing after a fight or after dying; it can also be ignored entirely. Lead hero designer Alec Dawson said players crave slightly more complexity than they had been given — [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/overwatch-2-perks.md)
- **Valorant:** four abilities per agent (signature, two purchased, ultimate) — [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/valorant-character-guide/); [Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/se-en/agents-valorant)
- **Valorant:** Ryan "Morello" Scott reportedly said damaging abilities mainly create threats, with guns providing most kills — [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/valorant-riot-games-give-take-damaging-abilities-overall-ability-design)
- **Valorant:** a founding roster was built around basic tactical functions (vision blocking, breaching, flushing, distracting), and many abilities were tried and scrapped — [The Spike](https://www.thespike.gg/valorant/news/valorant-developers-on-creating-agent-abilities/232)
- **Team Fortress 2:** the Direct Hit is a loadout swap rather than an in-fight mode. Rockets are 80% faster (about 1980 Hu/s) with 25% more damage and 70% less blast radius — [TF2 Wiki](https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Direct_Hit)
- **Monster Hunter Rise: Sunbreak:** Switch Skill Swap prepares two scrolls of skills out of combat, then flips between them in fight with a chord — [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/guides/how-use-switch-skill-swap-monster-hunter-rise-sunbreak/)

### Inferences
- **Recommendation from this evidence: each slug should have one signature ability in play, with any 1-3 options chosen outside the fight, as loadout or variant rather than per shot.** The slug choice already is the ability choice, in the way a Valorant ability key or a TF2 launcher is. A pre-shot selector stacks a second decision on the same moment.
- Keller's remark suggests a useful rule: if a slug wants three abilities, consider whether it should be three slugs. A collection game benefits from this anyway.
- A rough load comparison: a Valorant-style kit gives about four ability keys per agent, each a single press. A per-slug selector with a roster of N slugs and three abilities creates up to 3N outcomes behind two sequential choices. This is reasoning, not measurement.
- The owner's worry is supported by reasoning and one reviewer's experience. It has not been playtested in Slugshot, so a quick prototype comparison would be the real test.

### Gaps
- No primary developer explanation was found for why Splatoon uses fixed kits. Famitsu, 4Gamer or EDGE interviews may have it.
- No GDC talk specifically on ability count was found. Keller's GDC talk mentioned in search covered live service and team size, not kits.
- **Not researched to a citable standard:** Apex Legends kits, Destiny 2 elements and perks, Borderlands elemental guns, Pokemon-style move selection, and Risk of Rain. Treat any claims about these as unverified.

## Ability depth without a selector menu

### Takeaway
Several patterns were found with sources (charge, out-of-match variants, passively charged abilities), while others (detonate on second press, surface-dependent results) have no retrieved evidence and rest on reasoning alone.

### Cited Findings
- **Charge or hold:** Hanzo's bow ranges from 25 m/s at minimum charge to 110 m/s at maximum, giving one button a continuous range of outcomes. Figures are wiki values and may depend on patch — [Overwatch Wiki: Hanzo](https://overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Hanzo)
- **Out-of-match variants:** TF2's Direct Hit changes the feel of the same role through a loadout item — [TF2 Wiki](https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Direct_Hit)
- **Alternate kits:** Splatoon reuses a main with different sub and special pairings — [Nintendo Wire](https://nintendowire.com/news/2017/02/13/famitsu-interview-reveals-new-splatoon-2-details/)
- **Passive unlock:** Overwatch perks arrive through play and only augment existing abilities, though the mid-fight pick is the weak point — [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/overwatch-2-perks.md)
- **Charged by other play:** *Slug it Out 2* charges signature attacks through puzzle moves rather than per-attack menus — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id1148766137)
- **Two prepared sets:** Sunbreak's two scrolls are a precedent for a one-action toggle between presets, although its chord is awkward — [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/guides/how-use-switch-skill-swap-monster-hunter-rise-sunbreak/)

### Inferences
Options ranked by my judgment of fit for Slugshot. None has been tested:
1. **Out-of-match variant** (pick one of up to 3 forms when equipping the slug). This meets the owner's wish for choice at zero in-fight input cost.
2. **Hold versus tap on the same fire button.** It adds no new key. A hold also fits the canon, because charging can read as building speed, and the transformation could happen only at full charge. It costs time to fire, which should be tuned to feel like a choice rather than a delay.
3. **Context-sensitive results** based on what is hit (player, wall, floor). This requires no input at all and could suit Slugshot's readable-creature approach, but players must be able to predict it.
4. **Second press while the slug is in flight** (detonate or release). It uses one extra press only for players who want it, but requires a very short flight time to matter, which conflicts with fast shots.
5. **Combo effects between two slugs.** This gives depth from choosing and ordering shots, without new controls, and rewards the existing slug swap. Canon "tandem shots" may be a thematic hook, subject to verification.

Avoid: a per-shot menu, or any choice that must be made during a fight in the manner of Overwatch perks.

### Gaps
- No sourced examples were retrieved for detonate-on-second-press or surface-dependent results. Known examples probably exist, but none are cited here.
- No retention or usability data comparing these patterns was found.

## Time-to-input numbers and swap friction

### Takeaway
No rigorous measurements were found. What exists is anecdotal and player-reported, so treat it as direction only, and measure Slugshot directly.

### Cited Findings
- Hick's Law, formulated by Hick and Hyman in 1952: decision time grows with the number and complexity of options — [O'Reilly: Laws of UX](https://oreilly.com/library/view/laws-of-ux/9781098146955/ch04.html)
- Weapon wheels are the most recognisable radial menu in shooters — [300Mind](https://300mind.studio/blog/radial-menus-in-game-design/)
- Gameplay typically stops while a weapon wheel is open — [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/the-evolution-of-accessibility-weapons-wheel-f8fed0fed78e)
- Swapping trades damage for the time spent in the weapon animation — [West Games calculator, low authority](https://west-games.com/weapon-swap-dps-calculator/)
- Warframe player measurement: 2.0 s primary to secondary, 1.6 s the reverse, arguing for a maximum of about 0.5 s. Player-reported, and may be outdated — [Warframe forums](https://forums.warframe.com/topic/844731-weapon-swap-speed-buff-needed-those-numbers-tell-why)
- Apex players found that crouching during a swap makes the weapon ready sooner, which suggests players notice and try to cut small delays — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/apex-legends/how-to-switch-weapons-faster-in-apex-legends-377122)
- Counter-Strike poster's draw times of 2.2-2.7 s look high, are unverified and may be misreported. Not recommended as a benchmark — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/730/discussions/0/359543951730273530)
- Overwatch perk selection is awkward mid-fight even with only two options and one keybind — [Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/overwatch-2-perks.md)

### Inferences
- A wheel's usual cost, pausing or slowing play, cannot be paid in real-time PvP, so a selector would need live input during combat.
- A pre-shot selector adds a decision as well as a motion, so its cost is more than a keypress. The perks example suggests even a two-option pick is a problem under fire.
- A suggested internal target, not a sourced benchmark: slug switch to ready-to-fire under about 0.5 s, and no additional step between choosing a slug and firing it.

### Gaps
- No comparable official swap-time figures were found for Apex, Valorant or Overwatch. Measure in training ranges if needed.
- No study was found on the time cost of ability selection under combat load.

## Projectile speed, hitscan feel and Roblox limits

### Takeaway
Real speeds range from about 40 m/s (rockets) to 110-140 m/s (the fastest arrows). Canon's 100 mph (44.7 m/s) is therefore **rocket speed, not near-hitscan**. Near-hitscan feel needs a higher speed or short ranges, and on Roblox a fast projectile needs swept casts rather than Touched events.

### Cited Findings
Speeds (patch-dependent, mostly wiki or forum values):
- Pharah Rocket Launcher 40 m/s, Barrage 45 m/s — [Overwatch Wiki: Pharah](https://overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Pharah)
- Hanzo 25-110 m/s by charge; Storm Arrows possibly 140 m/s (the source layout is ambiguous) — [Overwatch Wiki: Hanzo](https://overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Hanzo)
- Hanzo was raised from 85 to 100 m/s in a 2018 patch, showing speed used as a tuning lever — [Blizzard patch notes, May 2018](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/all-overwatch-patch-notes-may-3-2018/87211)
- Genji shuriken 60 m/s (forum figure) — [Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/genji-needs-80-m-s-projectiles-125-dmg-sword-swings/172557)
- Zenyatta 90 m/s and Lucio 50 m/s, older forum figures — [Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/hanzos-projectiles-are-not-that-fast/340118)
- TF2 Direct Hit about 1980 Hu/s, which the wiki equates to 84 mph. So the Direct Hit, a "very fast rocket", is slower than the canon 100 mph threshold — [TF2 Wiki](https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Direct_Hit)

Balance:
- Direct Hit pattern: speed and damage are paid for with a much smaller blast (70% less radius), so it must actually connect — [TF2 Wiki](https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Direct_Hit)
- Hitscan removes any chance to dodge, which one developer calls unfair from the target's point of view — [Crossout](https://crossout.net/news/77)
- Dodgeability depends on projectile speed and size relative to the target's movement speed and size, and projectile speed should scale with character speed — [Critical Points](https://critpoints.net/2016/07/27/hitscan-versus-projectile-weapons/)
- Close-range shots with fast projectiles behave almost like hitscan because travel time is negligible — [Aimlabs](https://aimlabs.com/articles/?p=4245)
- A player model: a projectile's expected damage equals hitscan damage times the chance the target fails to dodge, so projectiles at best match hitscan unless otherwise compensated (forum opinion, not a developer) — [Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/should-projectile-heroes-do-more-damage/563810/73)
- Quake's high-DPS Plasma Gun is used for area denial and covering fire while the hitscan Lightning Gun stays top tier, which shows that DPS alone doesn't decide value — [Critical Points](https://critpoints.net/2016/07/27/hitscan-versus-projectile-weapons/)

Roblox:
- Community pattern: client simulates the projectile for visuals while the server does hit checks, sometimes with FastCast on both sides. Sync data must still be sent to clients — [DevForum: Server side hit detection](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/server-side-hit-detection/1295976); [DevForum: Is fast-cast worth it?](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/is-fast-cast-worth-it/962489)
- `Touched` is reported as unreliable for fast projectiles, and a projectile can pass its final hit position before a client-server round trip finishes — [DevForum: Server-side hit detection without Touched](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/server-side-projectile-hit-detection-without-touched-event/4195768)
- Per-frame CFrame movement can skip thin parts between checks. Suggested fixes are WorldRoot Raycast, Blockcast, Spherecast or Shapecast — [DevForum: Projectiles / Raycasting issues](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/projectiles-raycasting-issues/778533); [DevForum: Handling Railgun Bullets](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/handling-railgun-bullets/637935)
- FastCast raycasts each physics step to give an exact arc, but brings manual replication work — [DevForum: Is fast-cast worth it?](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/is-fast-cast-worth-it/962489)
- `UnreliableRemoteEvent` suits frequently changing, non-critical data, trading ordering and reliability for performance (read via the Turkish docs page) — [Roblox Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tr-tr/scripting/events/remote)
- A reported issue: `FireAllClients` on these events can log discards for clients that have not yet connected — [DevForum bug report](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/unreliableremoteeventfireallclients-still-attempts-invocations-on-non-connected-clients-contrary-to-docs/3852670)

### Inferences
Arithmetic from the cited speeds:

| Speed | Example | Time over 30 m | Time over 60 m |
|---|---|---|---|
| 40 m/s | Pharah | 0.75 s | 1.50 s |
| 44.7 m/s | Canon 100 mph | 0.67 s | 1.34 s |
| 60 m/s | Genji | 0.50 s | 1.00 s |
| 110 m/s | Hanzo at full charge | 0.27 s | 0.55 s |

- **Literal canon speed will feel like a rocket, not "almost hitscan".** If the owner wants near-hitscan, the game should be nearer 110+ m/s or use short engagement ranges. The name "100 mph" can stay as flavour without being the true simulated speed.
- If the transformation is meant to be seen, the slug should cross the threshold visibly close to the muzzle. Very high speeds leave little time to see a transformed creature, so the transform moment probably needs a deliberate VFX or brief slow start. This is a key tension to prototype.
- A plausible balance recipe for near-hitscan slugs, following the Direct Hit: high speed and high single-hit value, paid for with a slow fire rate, small hit radius, low ammo or a return delay. Canon's slug-return idea could naturally act as the reload, if verified.
- Roblox approach suggested by the community evidence: the firing client shows the shot at once, the server validates using swept casts (Spherecast fits a creature-sized projectile), and other clients get a cosmetic replay. Use a seeded origin, direction and time rather than streaming positions.
- Tunnelling scale: at an assumed 60 Hz step, 110 m/s is about 1.8 m per step. Under the commonly quoted 1 stud = 0.28 m (unsourced here), that is about 6.5 studs per step, well beyond Slugshot's roughly 0.93-stud ground slugs, so every step must be a swept cast.

### Gaps
- No official Roblox figures were retrieved for Heartbeat rate, physics replication rate, remote event limits or stud-to-metre scale. Check the English creator docs.
- No official source was found for Roblox's newer Server Authority model. Third-party material describes client prediction with server rollback, distinct from `SetNetworkOwner`, and should be verified before design relies on it.
- No Soldier: 76 or other confirmed hitscan comparison was retrieved, and no rigorous formula linking speed to damage or fire rate was found. Overwatch figures are mixed-date.
- Slugshot's real movement speed and engagement ranges were not reviewed here. They determine which speed feels right, per the Critical Points scaling argument.

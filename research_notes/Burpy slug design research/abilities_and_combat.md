# Burpy (Infurnus, fire slug): abilities and combat behaviour

> **Evidence quality: read this first.** No Burpy or Infurnus wiki page could be read in full. slugterra.fandom.com returned HTTP 402 and the breezewiki.discard.no mirror returned a bot-protection denial, and these blocks were not bypassed. All wiki facts below come from **search-engine summaries of those pages**, not from the pages themselves, so wording and attribution may be imperfect. The only full pages read were the Wikipedia episode list and one TheTVDB episode page. No episodes were watched.
>
> **Labels:** [SHOW-2] = episode synopsis on Wikipedia/TheTVDB (secondary source for show canon). [WIKI] = fan wiki, seen through a search summary. [FAN] = forum post or ranking list. [GAME] = game canon. [INFERENCE] = my own reasoning.

## Named or described attacks

### Takeaway
The fan wiki gives Infurnus a broad kit of about four core moves (shield, piercing shot, area trap, rideable flight) plus partner-specific fusion shots. Most of these names are wiki-level and not confirmed as spoken in the show. Only the Burpy + Joules fusion is backed by an episode synopsis.

### Cited Findings
**Species moves [WIKI, search summary]**
- Heatshield: the slug halts in mid-air, spreads flame wings and projects a protective wall of fire — [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)
- Spikescorch: a very hot micro-comet projectile, good at piercing stone or steel — [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)
- Flamespire: spiral rings of fire surround an opponent — [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)
- Updraft: flames turn blue, letting the slinger ride on the slug — [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus). The Burpy page labels the name as unconfirmed — [Burpy mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Burpy)
- The move lists differ by copy: the Fandom page reportedly adds Novaclaw and Wingburst, while the mirror lists Infernoslam. No descriptions of Novaclaw or Wingburst were retrieved — [Fandom Infurnus](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Infurnus); [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)

**Fusion shots [WIKI, search summary]**
- Novabolt (Infurnus + Tazerling; Burpy + Joules is the named pairing): an ionised plasma blast wrapped in flame — [Burpy mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Burpy)
- Nitrobubble (+ Bubbaleone): a flaming bubble — [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)
- Supernova (+ Phosphoro): the slugs spin while the Phosphoro lights up and the Infurnus amplifies the light — [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)
- Magmastrike (+ Armashelt): the pair launch a flaming boulder — [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)
- Infernoslam (+ Rammstone): the slugs orbit each other and produce a Rammstone with blazing horns — [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)

**Show support [SHOW-2]**
- In "The Unbeatable Master" (S2), Eli beats Blakk with a fusion shot using Burpy and Joules — [Wikipedia episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)
- In "The Hard Part", Eli and Kord work toward a two-slug fusion shot and need a missing blaster part — [TheTVDB](https://www.thetvdb.com/series/slugterra/episodes/4530651)
- In "The Return" (S3), Eli learns a double-barrel fusion shot and destroys Blakk's Gattler train. In "Light as Day" (S3), a fusion shot defeats a Megamorph — [Wikipedia episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] The fusion pattern is useful for design: the partner supplies the form (bubble, boulder, horns, light) and Infurnus adds heat. This is a good source of inspiration that need not copy any named shot.
- [INFERENCE] The kit spans defence, piercing, area denial and mobility, which suggests Infurnus is written as an all-rounder rather than a pure damage slug.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm which move names are spoken on screen, or which episode each move first appears in. Treat them all as wiki labels.
- No description of Novaclaw or Wingburst, and no named Burpy + Joo-Joo shot, was found. The absence is not proof that none exists.
- The Burpy + Joules fusion in "The Unbeatable Master" appears before the fusion development in "The Hard Part" and "The Return" in list order. I could not resolve this from synopses.

## Protoform versus velocimorph, and non-combat uses

### Takeaway
The protoform is a small utility flame, while the velocimorph is a phoenix-dragon-like combat form. Show synopses also show Burpy being useful outside combat, as a burner, a social go-between and a leader.

### Cited Findings
- Protoform: a small flame used for light in dark places, signal flares and lighting campfires [WIKI] — [SlugNet Burpy or Fandom Burpy, search summary; exact page attribution uncertain](https://slugnet.fandom.com/wiki/Burpy)
- Velocimorph: takes on phoenix-dragon hybrid traits [WIKI] — [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)
- Appearance: Infurnus are dark orange with yellow and tan markings, and Burpy has flame designs on his head and stomach [WIKI] — [Burpy mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Burpy)
- Ridden flight: the slinger rides it without pain even though the wings keep burning [WIKI] — [Infurnus mirror, search summary; exact page attribution uncertain](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)
- Burning an object: in "A Distant Shore", Eli burns a stolen journal with Burpy [SHOW-2] — [Wikipedia episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)
- Sealing a route: in "Light as Day", Burpy seals the entrance to the surface [SHOW-2] — [Wikipedia episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)
- Communication: in "Shadows and Light", Burpy's chirping persuades the Shadow Clan to let the gang through. In "Inheritance", he urges Eli to wear the Shadow Talker [SHOW-2] — [Wikipedia episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)
- Independent leadership: in "The Journey Home", Burpy leads Eli's stranded slugs, and they steal an elephant Mecha beast [SHOW-2] — [Wikipedia episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] The sharp contrast between a harmless campfire flame and a large combat form is a strong design hook: the same creature reads as both a pet and a weapon.
- [INFERENCE] The sources do not say how Burpy sealed the entrance in "Light as Day". Melting or collapse would be a guess.

### Gaps
- No source retrieved mentions cooking or heating. One low-quality page was reported to claim he melts solid rock, but I could not open it ([enviroliteracy.org](https://enviroliteracy.org/animals/is-burpy-a-elemental-slug/), HTTP 403). The closest supported claim is Spikescorch piercing stone or steel.

## How it travels when fired

### Takeaway
The transformation speed is well sourced at 100 mph. Infurnus-specific flight details, and what happens after impact, could not be sourced.

### Cited Findings
- A blaster shot takes a slug to 100 mph, at which point it becomes a stronger velocimorph form that usually resembles its protoform [WIKI] — [Slugs, search summary](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugs)
- Flopper slugs are reported as the only known slugs that do not transform at that speed [WIKI] — [Slugs, search summary](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugs)
- Heatshield implies an Infurnus can stop in mid-air rather than only flying straight [WIKI] — [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)
- Slugs are described as able to withstand severe impacts [WIKI] — [Slugs, search summary](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugs)
- Megamorph speed conflicts between sources. One account says a mega velocimorph comes at 200 mph, with the protoform glowing at 100 mph as a speed indicator. Another describes Burpy's first megamorph as being shot while Mecha upgrades allowed travel at 100 mph [WIKI, conflict] — [Megamorph, search summary](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Megamorph)
- Mega Morphing is discovered in "Upgrade" (S3), using new Mecha Beasts [SHOW-2] — [Wikipedia episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] The two megamorph claims may be compatible if the Mecha's 100 mph adds to the blaster's 100 mph to make 200 mph. No source retrieved states this.

### Gaps
- **Not sourced:** trajectory, spin, flame trail, Burpy's top speed and the visual effect on impact.
- **Not sourced:** whether a slug reverts to protoform after impact and returns to its slinger. Search results did not address this. My unverified recollection is that slugs do revert and return, but it needs checking against an episode before anyone relies on it.

## Strengths, weaknesses and Ghoul changes

### Takeaway
Infurnus is framed as rare and elite, with water and some air-style deflection cited as counters, but the matchup evidence is thin. Burpy's ghoul history is better attested, and the Doc-type healer slug is the recurring cure. No game statistics were found.

### Cited Findings
**Standing**
- Infurnus are described as ultimate fire-based weapons and master strategists [WIKI] — [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)
- Infurnus are associated with the champion and protector of a set of caverns, with Joo-Joo as the Eastern Caverns' Infurnus, owned by Junjie [WIKI] — [Joo-Joo](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Joo-Joo)
- Burpy is reported as the first known megamorph, though the Joo-Joo page claims Joo-Joo megamorphed over 2000 years earlier [WIKI, conflict] — [Megamorph](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Megamorph); [Joo-Joo](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Joo-Joo)
- Burpy is not an Elemental slug but an Infurnus, per a low-reliability page seen only through search — [enviroliteracy.org](https://enviroliteracy.org/animals/is-burpy-a-elemental-slug/)
- A fan ranking says the Earth Elemental knocked out a Mega Morph Infurnus in "Slug Fu Showdown" [FAN] — [TheTopTens, search summary](https://www.thetoptens.com/television/slugterra-slugs/)

**Counters**
- An Aquabeek's Aquajet is said to beat an Infurnus easily, because it is water-based [WIKI] — [Aquabeek mirror, search summary; exact page attribution uncertain](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Aquabeek)
- A Tempesto's Revecto is also said to beat it easily [WIKI] — [Infurnus mirror, search summary; exact page attribution uncertain](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)

**Limits**
- In "Lightwell" (S3), Burpy falls ill and Eli takes him to the Lightwell for help [SHOW-2] — [Wikipedia episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)
- A "neo-protoform" state is described, in which a drained slug or ghoul shrinks to a baby-like form. It is not tied to ordinary firing [WIKI] — [Slugs, search summary](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugs)

**Ghoul and Dark Water**
- Dark Water is what Blakk uses to make ghouls, as learned in "Deadweed" [SHOW-2] — [Wikipedia episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)
- Darkfurnus is the wiki's name for a ghouled Infurnus, described as extremely rare and powerful. Its moves include Darkpyre, a dark fireball that grows as it travels [WIKI/FAN] — [Darkfurnus discussion](https://slugterra.fandom.com/f/t/Darkfurnus)
- "What Lies Beneath": Burpy is tagged as a temporary ghoul, and a fan post says the gang ghouled their slugs deliberately so they could fire them [FAN, unverified] — [Ghouled Infurnus post](https://slugterra.fandom.com/f/p/2023803546763770953)
- "Ghoul from Beyond": a Goon Doc ghouls Burpy on collision and he turns on the gang. Eli fires Doc to restore him. Later, Burpy and Doc enter Eli's mind, where Burpy fights the Goon while Doc heals Eli [WIKI] — [Ghoul from Beyond](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugterra:_Ghoul_from_Beyond); [Doc](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Doc)
- "Return of the Elementals": Burpy is ghouled when the Fire Elemental falls to the Goon. Once restored, he seems confused but happy to see Will Shane, and later returns to Eli's shoulder [WIKI] — [Burpy mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Burpy)
- Slug Energy reportedly turns Burpy blue with blue fire [WIKI, wording unclear in summary] — [Burpy mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Burpy)

**Game canon**
- Slugterra: Slug it Out 2 uses Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Energy and Psychic elements, with over 100 slugs [GAME, store listing via search] — [TapTap](https://www.taptap.io/app/87713)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] "Water beats fire" is presented as an easy win, but on the evidence here it comes from species-page matchup notes, not from a stated rule of the show.
- [INFERENCE] Corruption flips loyalty rather than removing power, so a "corrupted" variant works best as an opposing behaviour rather than a stat penalty.

### Gaps
- No Infurnus numbers from Slug it Out or Dark Waters were found: no stats, damage values or in-game weak/strong tables. I could not verify that the games apply fire-versus-water as the wiki does.
- No sourced statement on tiredness or a recharge time after firing.
- "Dark Water" changes specific to Burpy are not separately documented. The sources describe ghouling by Goon influence and, per a fan post, deliberate ghouling.

## Source conflicts and what is fan inference

### Takeaway
Treat the whole move list as wiki-sourced. The episode synopses support plot events, not mechanics or move names.

### Cited Findings
- **First megamorph:** Burpy versus Joo-Joo — [Megamorph](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Megamorph); [Joo-Joo](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Joo-Joo)
- **Megamorph speed:** 200 mph versus a 100 mph Mecha account — [Megamorph](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Megamorph)
- **Move list:** differs between the Fandom page and the mirror — [Fandom Infurnus](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Infurnus); [Infurnus mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)
- **Season numbering:** "The Hard Part" is S2 E11 on Wikipedia but S2 E12 on TheTVDB (overall 25 on both) — [Wikipedia episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes); [TheTVDB](https://www.thetvdb.com/series/slugterra/episodes/4530651)
- **Format:** "Ghoul from Beyond" and "Return of the Elementals" are described as films by the wiki but listed as S4 multi-part episodes on Wikipedia — [Ghoul from Beyond](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugterra:_Ghoul_from_Beyond); [Wikipedia episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)
- **Ghouling cause:** "Ghoul from Beyond" describes ghouling by collision with a Goon Doc, whereas "Return of the Elementals" ties it to the Fire Elemental's fall. These may be separate events — [Ghoul from Beyond](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Slugterra:_Ghoul_from_Beyond); [Burpy mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Burpy)

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Move names such as Spikescorch are specific third-party IP labels. An original design should use the functions as loose inspiration and avoid the names, the phoenix-dragon form and the specific fusion pairings.

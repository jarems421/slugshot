# Build the fire slug around speed, not Burpy

Burpy (you wrote "burpee", but every source spells it Burpy) is a rare fire-type Infurnus slug, the loyal companion the hero Eli inherits from his father. When fired he changes from a small orange slug into a burning phoenix-dragon. The canon transform speed is **100 mph, not 100 ft/s**, and 100 mph is rocket speed, so your near-hitscan feel needs a shot **about 3 times faster than canon or more** (proposal: 500 studs per second as a first test). My recommendation is **one signature ability per slug in play**, with up to three variants picked outside the fight and never per shot. For the name I suggest **Scorchet**, with Cinderwick as the backup, and I would avoid "Inferno". To stay original, change the transformed shape as well as the colours: no wings, no bird-dragon. All gameplay numbers here are proposals to prototype, none are approved, and nothing has been playtested.

**How to read the labels.** **[Show]** comes from Wikipedia's plot and series summaries, which is the best evidence available, since nobody watched the episodes. **[Wiki]** is fan-wiki material seen only through search summaries, because the wiki and its mirror were blocked, so its wording and page attribution may be off. **[Design]** is my own reasoning.

## Burpy is a pet-sized flame that becomes a burning beast

**[Show]** Slugs are tiny creatures with supernatural powers, trained as living ammunition for blasters, and they turn into powerful beasts on reaching 100 mph ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra)). Burpy's plot moments rest on character as much as firepower: his chirping talks the gang past the Shadow Clan, he leads Eli's stranded slugs on a heist, he falls ill, and a fusion shot with Joules beats the villain Blakk ([episode list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Slugterra_episodes)).

**[Wiki]** Burpy was Will Shane's slug, sent to find Will's son Eli just before Will disappeared. He is described as extremely loyal, clever and Eli's number-one slug ([Burpy](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Burpy)). The species is rare, limited to one per set of caverns, and goes to each region's champion. The small form is dark orange with yellow and tan markings, a tan flame-patterned belly, yellow antenna tips and a yellow mark on the head. The transformed form has phoenix-dragon traits, with burning wings and tail ([Infurnus](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Infurnus)). Its listed moves cover a fire shield, a piercing micro-comet, spiral rings of fire around an opponent and a rideable flight, though the lists differ between copies ([mirror, seen via search](https://breezewiki.discard.no/slugterra/wiki/Infurnus)). A second tier, the megamorph, comes at 200 mph and is a permanent change ([Megamorph](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Megamorph)). The sources disagree on whether the head mark is a flame or a lightning bolt, and on whether Burpy was the first to megamorph ([Joo-Joo](https://slugterra.fandom.com/wiki/Joo-Joo)). I found no canon statement that he is the most powerful slug.

**Unknown from every source reached:** what happens on impact, how a slug reverts and returns to its slinger, whether Burpy spins in flight, and whether one slug can choose between shots. Spin is therefore your own idea and free to use. The wiki's several moves per species suggest canon slugs have range, but nothing explains how a shot gets chosen, so canon does not settle your ability question.

## Canon speed is a rocket, so near-hitscan needs far more

**100 mph is about 45 m/s**, close to Pharah's 40 m/s rockets in Overwatch ([Overwatch Wiki](https://overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Pharah)). TF2's "very fast" Direct Hit rocket is about 84 mph ([TF2 Wiki](https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Direct_Hit)). Even Hanzo's fully charged arrow, at 110 m/s, is about 2.5 times canon speed and is still a dodgeable projectile ([Overwatch Wiki](https://overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Hanzo)). What counts is shot speed relative to the target's movement ([Critical Points](https://critpoints.net/2016/07/27/hitscan-versus-projectile-weapons/)), so the table below uses your experimental 30 studs-per-second sprint (`experiments/movement/shared/Config.luau`).

| Shot speed (studs/s) | Time to cross 100 studs | Sprinting target moves | Distance per 60 Hz step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 160 (canon 100 mph) | 0.63 s | 19 studs | 2.7 studs |
| 300 | 0.33 s | 10 studs | 5 studs |
| **500 (suggested first test)** | **0.20 s** | **6 studs** | **8.3 studs** |
| 800 | 0.13 s | 4 studs | 13 studs |

**[Design]** This is arithmetic, not a measurement. It assumes 1 stud = 0.28 m, which is a commonly quoted figure I have not verified, and 100 studs is an illustrative range because real fight distances are not yet known. I also found no set Floppy shot speed in the repo, so "much faster than Floppy" still needs a measured baseline. At these speeds every step must use a swept cast, because community reports say `Touched` is unreliable for fast shots and stepped movement skips thin parts ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/projectiles-raycasting-issues/778533)). A common pattern is for the firing client to show the shot immediately while the server checks hits ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/server-side-hit-detection/1295976)).

**[Design] The main tension is that at 500 studs/s the transformation is barely visible.** The first 30 studs of flight last about 4 frames at 60 fps. I would not fix this with a slow start, because that kills the near-hitscan feel. Instead, make the change a burst at the muzzle, draw the flight as a corkscrew flame streak so the spin still reads, and let the big form appear for a brief moment at impact. That moment is where players will actually see the creature.

## One ability in play is the right call, and your worry has evidence behind it

**Fixed kits chosen before play are the norm among the games checked.** Splatoon sells weapons as set bundles and offers variety through alternative kits ([Nintendo Wire](https://nintendowire.com/news/2017/02/13/famitsu-interview-reveals-new-splatoon-2-details/)), and TF2 changes the feel of a weapon through a loadout swap. The closest evidence for your worry is Overwatch's perks. Picking between just two options with one key was reported as hard to do mid-fight, and the reviewer advises choosing after the fight ([Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/overwatch-2-perks.md)). Overwatch's director has also said large kits can get a little overwhelming ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/overwatch-2/hero-complexity)). The evidence is partial, though: one reviewer's experience and no study of selection time under fire.

**[Design]** In Slugshot, choosing the slug already is choosing the ability, so a per-shot selector adds a second decision to the same moment. The other side deserves a fair hearing, since Overwatch's lead hero designer says players crave slightly more complexity (same Aftermath piece). So I would ship **one ability per slug first**, then add **up to three variants chosen when equipping the slug** if the game needs more choice. If a slug seems to want three abilities in play, it probably wants to be three slugs, which also suits a collecting game. Depth can come from what the shot hits and from combining two slugs, neither of which adds a button. Hold-to-charge suits a future slug, but it would fight this one's fast feel. Keep the slug switch quick, with a suggested internal target of under about 0.5 s and no extra step before firing.

## A proposed original slug: Scorchet

**The name.** "Inferno" is risky for three reasons: it sounds like Infurnus, one Wikipedia summary spells the species "Infernus" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slugterra:_Ghoul_from_Beyond)), and a listed fusion move is called Infernoslam. It is also a common word, so it would not feel like yours. **Scorchet** (scorch + rocket, with a hint of ricochet) says fire and speed in one word, and that is my pick. **Cinderwick** (a lit fuse) is warmer and more pet-like but sounds slower. Quick web searches for Scorchet and Cinderwick found no game or creature using either, though "Scorch" alone is used by several unrelated characters ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scorch)). That is a sanity check, not legal clearance. In-game, also avoid canon terms such as "velocimorph".

**The look. [Design]** Change several traits together, because a recolour would keep the same layout of head mark, belly mark and antenna tips. Try a charcoal, glossy body with glowing coal cracks and a smouldering vent ridge along the back, a plain belly, no emblem on the crown and distinct antenna tips. Dark ember also separates him from the burnt-orange blaster and suits the [style guide](../docs/art/style-guide.md)'s calm base with one vibrant accent. The transformed form should be a **wingless spinning comet with a corkscrew tail**, not a bird or dragon. The personality can be a hyperactive, impatient contrast to drowsy Floppy, who stays non-transforming as already [confirmed](../docs/slugs/catalog/flopper-inspired.md). A private side-by-side check with official Burpy art is still needed, since no images were viewed for this research.

**The balance. [Design]** Near-hitscan speed, heavy hits and rapid fire together would make a hitscan gun that targets cannot dodge, which one developer calls unfair from the target's side ([Crossout](https://crossout.net/news/77)). TF2's Direct Hit shows the fix: its speed and damage are paid for with 70% less blast radius, so the shot must really connect. I read "fires fast" as the shot leaving and arriving fast, not spraying. Starting proposals are a **small hit radius of about 0.5 studs** despite the big visual, **about 1.5 times Floppy's hit**, **0.6 to 0.8 s between shots**, **3 to 4 direct hits to down a player**, and **3 to 4 shots before a short reload**. The bright flame trail also gives away the shooter's position, which is a useful extra cost. Canon's slug return could become that reload, but I could not verify how it works in the show.

**The abilities. [Design]** These are in-house ideas that share only the general fire theme with canon. Avoid the canon names, the shield-and-fireball pairing, ring traps, ridden flight and the specific fusion pairings.

| Ability | What it does | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| **Kindle (recommended signature)** | The first hit marks the target. A second hit within about 2 s ignites them for bonus damage. | Makes hard hits something players earn, and the target can respond by breaking line of sight. |
| Burn line (variant) | The shot leaves a thin line of fire along its path for about 1 s. | Denies an area and makes a very fast shot readable. |
| Skip (variant) | The spin bounces the shot once off a hard surface, for reduced damage. | Uses the spin as a mechanic, but it must be predictable. |

## Conclusion

The research shifts the question from "how do we adapt Burpy?" to "what makes a shot you can barely see feel like a creature?" The muzzle burst, spin streak and impact moment will carry this slug's identity more than its flight model does, so test them first, alongside 300, 500 and 800 studs/s in a greybox range. A speed sweep and one ability are a small prototype that would answer most of this.

Three decisions from you would unblock it: the name, whether "fires fast" means fast flight or rapid fire, and whether one ability per slug is the rule. Before anyone relies on the canon details, someone with browser access should read the Burpy, Infurnus and Slugs wiki pages directly and check an episode for how slugs impact and return.
